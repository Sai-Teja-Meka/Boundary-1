## What this document is, and is not

`BOUNDARY.md` is frozen and has no amendment mechanism. **This document does not
amend it and cannot.** A sentence here that contradicted a ratified sentence of
`BOUNDARY.md` would be void on its face, and the constitutional procedure for a
wrong rule remains what `CLAUDE.md §5` says it is: log the objection and stop.

It is also **not** an `ATTAINABILITY.md` and not a substitute for one. It binds
no gate, names no corpus as a binding substrate, applies no threshold to any
engine, declares no gate constant, and leaves `trials/laws/t_rulings.py`'s
registry untouched. `core/layers/l8_self_description.py`,
`trials/adapters/l8.py` and every Layer-8 trial directory do not exist, and
`R2`'s standing step still orders them after the arithmetic.

What it *does* is the half of the Phase 3→4 gate that can be done before any
Layer-8 artifact exists: it fixes **what will be measured, over what
denominator, through which `§7` surface**, what a Layer-8 artifact **is**, what
the humility side **stands on**, which law seams are **settled** and which are
**reserved**, and in what **order** the rest happens. It names every instrument
and no range.

## Where the authority comes from

`BOUNDARY.md §5` states, in its own frozen text:

> Layers 8–9 receive laws but **not thresholds** yet — thresholds are
> **specified at the Phase 3→4 gate**.

and both rows of its table read *specified at Phase 3→4 gate* in the ascension
column **and** in the humility column, where every other row states both. `§5.1`
carries a threshold-defense paragraph for every layer from 1 to 7 and **none for
either of these two**, so the *"capability-specific quantities named in §5.1"*
that `§5`'s own preamble says a gate is stated in **do not exist** for Layer 8 or
Layer 9, and neither does a corpus precondition — of which `§5` states exactly
one anywhere, Layer 3's.

Two things follow, and the second is why this document has a §2 at all.

1. The constitution concedes that some gates are specified later, and that the
   specifying document is **not** `BOUNDARY.md`, because `BOUNDARY.md` is frozen
   and cannot receive them. `BOUNDARY-RULINGS.md`'s preamble derives its own
   authority from exactly that concession and names this file.
2. **Specifying a threshold requires specifying what it is a threshold on.**
   Since `§5.1` names no quantity for either layer, naming the quantities falls
   inside the same concession — it is not an addition to it. That is this
   document's §2, and it is the only place in the ladder where a quantity a gate
   cites was not already written down by the constitution.

## §0. The ordering collision, and the resolution

The post-L7 `PULSE` named this as the single biggest risk to the Phase 3→4 gate
(`BOUNDARY.log` line 48), of exactly the species line 28 met at Layer 5 —
**constitutional rather than corpus**:

* `§5` schedules the Layer-8 and Layer-9 **thresholds** at the Phase 3→4 gate;
* `R2` — a ruling that binds *"at every layer, including `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md` when
  it is written"* — requires a gate's discrimination arithmetic to be **computed
  and recorded in an `ATTAINABILITY.md` before the gate binds**, and states
  **attainability precedes authority** as a standing step;
* at the moment the constitution schedules this document there is **no Layer-8
  artifact, no Layer-8 measure and therefore no arithmetic that could be
  recorded**.

A `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md` that stated a binding Layer-8 threshold at this moment
would breach `R2` in the act of scheduling it: it would fix a number before
anyone could know whether it lies strictly below what the capability can reach
and strictly above what capability-free policy can reach — the two things `R2`
obligation 1 and obligation 2 exist to establish. And `R2` is not waivable here
as a matter of course: a supplement is superseded only by a **later entry that
says so**, which is a decision and not a default, and no such entry exists.

**The resolution, and it is the whole shape of this document.** `§5`'s deferral
is discharged by specifying the gates **structurally** and deferring each
threshold **explicitly, by name, in this document's own text** to that layer's
Stage A plus a ruling. Two readings of `§5`'s sentence were available and the
second is taken:

* **Reading A — the gate is one session and states numbers.** Under it this
  document would have to name integers now. It is refused: it is the reading
  under which `§5` and `R2` cannot both be honoured, and under which the very
  first act of the document would be the discrimination failure the whole
  ladder's ruling stock exists to prevent. A number invented here would have no
  artifact, no measure and no baseline behind it — *"a number in a table, not a
  test"*, in `R2` obligation 4's own words.
* **Reading B — the Phase 3→4 gate is a PROCEDURE, and this document is its
  law.** `§5` says thresholds *"are specified at the Phase 3→4 gate"*; it does
  not say the gate is one session, does not name a filename, and does not say
  the specification must be an integer written before its arithmetic. Under
  Reading B the gate is: **this document, then each layer's Stage A, then that
  layer's ruling** — and a threshold is *specified at the gate* when it is
  ratified by the ruling this document schedules. This is also what `§5`'s own
  *"receive laws but not thresholds"* already describes, and what **every layer
  since Layer 3 has actually done**: the number in `§5` was ratified in one
  place and acquired its authority — its corpus, its reading, its denominators —
  in `R1`, `R4`, `R6`, `R7`, `R8`.

Reading B is taken. **No number is declared in this document.** The post-L7
`PULSE` predicted this shape and asked whether the gate is one session or two;
the answer recorded here is that it is **not one session** — it is this document
plus a Stage A plus a ruling **per layer**, and §6 is the schedule.

**The no-number property, stated so it can be checked rather than trusted.**

> Every cell of every `threshold` column in this document reads
> **`DEFERRED — Stage A + ruling`** and carries no digit; and no Layer-8 or
> Layer-9 gate constant exists anywhere under `trials/`, nor does any gate
> constant cite this document as its authority.

`trials/laws/t_boundary_high.py` asserts both halves. The deferral is therefore
mechanical: a session that wrote a Layer-8 threshold into a table here, or a
`GATE_*` / `CEILING_*` constant into a Layer-8 trial, turns the suite **red**
before it can turn a number into an authority.

## §2. The measures, before the thresholds

`§5` states a gate *"in the metric that layer is about — the four measures of §3,
plus the capability-specific quantities named in §5.1"*, and `§5.1` names none
for either layer. This section names them. It states, for each clause-to-be,
**what is measured, over what denominator, and through which `§7` surface** —
the instrument, even though its range is deferred.

Three disciplines govern every row and are not repeated in the rows themselves:

* **the denominator is the artifact's declared class** unless `R8` clause 3(a)'s
  two conditions both hold and the `ATTAINABILITY.md` says which — the harness
  can check every member against frozen bytes or a declared grammar, *and*
  another artifact-bound clause of the same gate makes shrinking the report
  costly;
* **the surface is `§7` alone.** Every quantity is read off an `Answer` returned
  by `query`, or off `snapshot`'s canonical bytes. Nothing is read out of engine
  bookkeeping. The standing precedent is `{"op":"fired","iid":I}` answering with
  a **list** because `dup-fire = 0` is a gate clause, and `autopsy/writ`'s
  out-of-band `getProvenance` probe is precisely the thing not to reproduce;
* **`R7` clause 2's declaration**: every battery states `A`, `n_pos` and `n_neg`
  beside any `§3.4` quantity, and declares **per class** whether that class
  scores inside the denominator.

**Why groundedness is the discriminating quantity, and why it is not fidelity.**
An engine can be *correct* about a question whose answer it holds in a field, and
`§5 L8` does not ask for that: it asks for introspection **from state**. What
separates a self-description from a field read is that its answer is a **fact
about the state that no single field carries** — a fold, a census, a claim about
what was lost. Groundedness is the quantity that can tell those apart, because
the derivation procedure computes the answer independently and the comparison is
against the derivation and never against the engine's own report. Fidelity
cannot do that work alone, for the reason §5.4 records: `§7.3` guarantees an
engine that has no self-model a **scored abstention** at 100 apiece, so `F` on
its own measures `§3.0`'s price list rather than the capability.

### 2.2 Self-description's key inheritance, stated

Two residuals the ladder carries are Layer 8's raw material rather than
background, and both are measurements this project has already taken on itself.

**THE NO-MODEL CLASS IS `§5 L8`'S SUBJECT.** `[L7] [DOGFOOD]`'s fall-through
census (`shell/dogfood/FIELD.md`, 2026-08-03; `BOUNDARY.log` lines 47–48)
classified every permille the surface prints by the branch of Layer 6's
`confidence_for` that produced it, and the class named `default:no-model` — an op
Layer 6 attaches `CERTAIN` to **by construction**, because it returns content it
regenerated exactly or abstains — is precisely the class of op `§5 L8` is about.
The count stands on the record at **650 of 9009 answers**, and it is quoted here
as an inherited **measurement**, not as a threshold: no gate is stated over it,
and any Layer-8 artifact restates the class from its own bytes rather than
importing that figure.

The consequence binds §2.1's calibration row and is the reason it is worded as it
is: **at Layer 8 the layer's own answers and the answers nobody has a confidence
model for are the same answers.** A calibration clause stated naively over that
class would measure a fall-through and not a model. So any Layer-8 calibration
clause must be stated over a class in which **a self-descriptive answer can be
wrong** — which is `R7` clause 3(b)'s forcing-region requirement, reached at this
layer by a different route, and which §3 makes the artifact's burden.

**THE FALL-THROUGH BRANCH CLASSIFICATION IS THE RAW MATERIAL.** The four branches
the census separated — `measured`, `default:not-set-once`, `default:no-model`,
`default:no-chain` — are an instrument for saying *which* certainty an engine is
stating and why, and a Layer-8 artifact inherits that classification as the
shape of its calibration class rather than inventing one. It is raw material and
not a law: `README-l7 §4` leaves the residual **open**, states that `ECE 0` on
`corpora/l7compose` is not evidence it is closed, and states what would close it
— an artifact on which the composed answer can be **wrong**. This document does
not close it either, and no session may cite this section as having closed it.

## §3. The substrate doctrine — what a Layer-8 artifact IS

**The problem, stated exactly.** A self-description's correct answers are a
function of **the engine under test**, not of frozen bytes. `§8.2` and `§8.3`
freeze a *generator's output* and enforce reproduction byte-for-byte; there is no
seeded generator for an engine's behaviour, so **the byte-match law cannot reach
a self-descriptive answer key**. This is the same shortage `§6`'s mandatory
Layer-7 self-pollution strain had, whose substrate is engine output, and which
the Layer-7 arc solved by **strain-computes / anchor-pins** without inventing a
corpus doctrine `§8` does not have. Layer 8 is that problem as the *whole*
layer rather than as one strain.

**The answer this document gives.** A Layer-8 artifact is not one object but a
**triple**, and only the first member is a corpus in `§8`'s sense:

1. **A FROZEN EVENT STREAM.** An ordinary `§8` corpus: a deterministic generator
   at an explicit seed drawing solely from `corpora/prng.py`, output frozen to a
   committed file, `§8.3`'s byte-match law binding unchanged, `§8.5`'s holdout
   range respected. Nothing here is new and nothing here is relaxed. The stream
   is what the engine under test ingests, and it is the only member of the triple
   the constitution already knows how to freeze.
2. **A FROZEN QUERY SET WITH A DECLARED CLASS TABLE.** The self-descriptive
   queries, and for each one the class it belongs to — which is the denominator
   `§2` binds every ratio to under `R8` clause 3. It ships in **one canonical
   JSON object together with the stream and the class table**, for
   `corpora/l6batteryb`'s reason carried forward: the guarantees are a **joint**
   property of the members, and three separately byte-matched files could be
   paired across generations while every individual check stayed green.
3. **A FROZEN DERIVATION PROCEDURE.** Committed source, engine-free, that maps
   *(the frozen stream, the engine's own frozen trace and canonical state)* to
   the expected answer for each query in the class. It is a **declared reading**
   in exactly the shape `ASSERTION_FORMS` (L4), `INTENTION_FORM` (L5),
   `SET_ONCE_KEYS` (L6) and `COMPOSITION_FORM` (L7) already have — with one
   difference that is the whole layer: those readings read the **caller's
   payloads**, and this one reads the **engine's snapshot**.

**The answer key is therefore a PROCEDURE and not a table**, and that is the
substrate doctrine in one sentence. What makes it legitimate is that the
procedure's output is still a deterministic function of frozen bytes:

* `§2.3` makes the engine's trace and final state **byte-reproducible** from the
  frozen stream — determinism is a law here and is enforced by the `laws/` class;
* `§7.1`'s `snapshot` is **pure** and returns **canonical** bytes (`§2.4`) that
  round-trip, so *"the engine's state"* is a well-defined byte string and not an
  implementation's private opinion;
* `§2.1`'s purity and `§2.2`'s prohibitions apply to the procedure as they do to
  anything the harness computes exactly: integers and `Fraction`, no float, no
  clock, no randomness.

So the key is **recomputed, not recorded**, and the artifact's obligations are
the same three the byte-match law gives an ordinary corpus, restated for this
shape:

| obligation | for an ordinary `§8` corpus | for a Layer-8 artifact |
|---|---|---|
| **freeze** | `§8.2`: the generator's output at a named `(seed, scale)` is frozen to a committed file | the **stream** freezes under `§8.2` unchanged; the **query set and class table** freeze inside the same canonical object; the **derivation procedure** freezes as committed source in a file of its own, superseded only by a later artifact and never edited in place (`§9.2`) |
| **hash** | `§8.8`'s checksum branch, for a corpus with no generator | the **derived key's canonical bytes carry a recorded `sha256`** in the artifact's manifest, and an `anchors/` entry pins it — the class `§6` gives this project for pinning exact behaviour that has no generator, which is what the Layer-7 arc used and is used here rather than minting a doctrine `§8` does not have |
| **reproduce** | `§8.3`: re-running the generator reproduces the file byte-for-byte, or the suite is red | re-running the **derivation** over the frozen stream reproduces the key **byte-for-byte**, asserted every run. It is stated as `§8.3`'s **analogue** and never claimed to *be* `§8.3`, because `§8.3` is about a generator and this is about a procedure over an engine. |

**Ground truth about an engine is then as tamper-evident as ground truth about a
corpus**, which is the requirement this section exists to meet: the stream is
byte-matched, the key is recomputed and hash-pinned, the procedure is frozen
source, and a drift in any of the three is red rather than disappointing.

**Four constraints on the procedure, each with the failure it prevents.**

* **It reads `snapshot`, never an `Answer`.** A key derived from what the engine
  *says* is `R8` clause 3's self-reported denominator with the roles swapped: the
  engine would be grading itself, and every score would be a tautology. The
  procedure reads the state's canonical bytes and the frozen stream, and nothing
  the engine says about either.
* **It is one reading with two implementations, and they are asserted to agree.**
  The engine's self-description and the harness's derivation compute the same
  quantity from the same bytes; an `ops/` trial asserts agreement query by query
  on the frozen artifact, in the shape `ops/l4`, `ops/l5`, `ops/l6` and `ops/l7`
  each established for their layer's declared reading. A divergence moves an
  **answer** and not merely an availability, which is why it is a trial and not a
  comment.
* **It may not import the engine.** Stage A's witness and the harness's
  derivation are checked against their own **source** — the `R8` clause 1
  precedent, where the class-E witness is forbidden to import the artifact, its
  generator, its answer key or the battery module, *because the generator is part
  of the answer key and not part of the substrate*.
* **Its class must contain questions no field carries.** A class made entirely of
  questions whose answers are single fields of the snapshot certifies the
  engine's layout and measures nothing — the Layer-8 analogue of `R7` clause 1's
  fourth substrate kill, where **on murk, evidence that ranks also resolves**,
  and of `R8` clause 1's fifth, where **not one answer was absent from its own
  stream**. §4 states the same requirement from the humility side.

**The discrimination burden this places on the artifact, and the predicted
sixth substrate kill.** `R2` obligation 2 needs a class of self-descriptive
question a capability-free engine cannot answer; `R7` clause 3(b) needs, for any
clause citing `AUROC`, that both classes non-empty is a **theorem the artifact
carries** and not a fact relative to a declared reading. Whether such a class is
constructible **at all** for a claim about an engine — where the engine holds the
state the question is about, so the resolving signal cannot simply be withheld
from it the way `corpora/l6batteryb` withholds a coin — is the arithmetic Stage A
owes, and it is **not obviously yes**. It is named in advance so a later session
can score it: the predicted sixth substrate kill is that **a question forced to
come from a fold may also be trivially answerable from a field**, and the fix
would have the shape the Layer-7 arc found — *put the class in the store rather
than in the query* — with no guarantee that shape transfers to a question whose
subject **is** the store.

**What §8.8 does and does not offer here.** `§8.8`'s real-data rule makes a
frozen, checksummed snapshot of real data a legal corpus and is available in
principle for a Layer-8 stream. It is **not** relied on by this document, and no
Layer-8 artifact may be premised on the scale of any real corpus: `[L7]
[PACKAGE]` **stopped** the `real-sessions/v2` freeze, `V2-FREEZE-STOPPED.md`
records the refusal with its three declined shortcuts, and that is a decision
about a frozen artifact awaiting **its own ruling**. This document does not
resolve it, does not extend `REFUSED_STOCK` to it, and reads no scale of it into
any section above.

## §4. The humility axes

`§6` requires, for every layer `N`, that the humility trial run **layer `N`'s own
ascension tasks** against `make_engine(layer_cap = N−1)` through the same generic
interface, assert the capped engine scores at or below the declared ceiling, and
ship an `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` giving a **structural** argument. `§5` states the
Layer-8 and Layer-9 ceilings as *specified at Phase 3→4 gate* — so unlike every
layer below, **the ceiling's measure is not stated even once** and this document
must define it. The number stays deferred.

### 4.1 The seam, and what answers it

`core/layers/README-l7.md §5` records the difficulty in its own words: *"the
humility ceiling has nowhere obvious to stand — `§6` caps at `N − 1` and a
capped-7 engine has every verb Layer 8 will use — `query` is `query`."*

**The answer is that the verb is not the vocabulary.** `§7.1` fixes three
operations and Layer 8 adds none (§5.2). What a Layer-8 engine adds is a class of
**query it can answer**, and `§7.3` is explicit about what a capped engine does
with a query type it does not support: it **abstains**, and the abstention is
**scored**, never thrown. So the capped engine's behaviour on Layer 8's own tasks
is well-defined without any special construction, and the humility side stands on
the **op vocabulary and the answer class**, not on the verb.

**And that fixes the shape of the ceiling measure, which is the one structural
decision this section makes.** `§3.0` pays an abstention **100**, so a ceiling
stated in `F` would sit near the abstention floor by construction and would
measure `§3.0`'s price list rather than the capability — vacuous in the precise
sense `R2`'s mirror forbids. Therefore:

> **A Layer-8 or Layer-9 humility ceiling is stated over a capability quantity
> whose numerator counts correct self-descriptions (or correct emissions), and
> never over `F`.** An abstention contributes **nothing** to such a numerator.

This is `R8` clause 7's move made general: the Layer-7 conjunction was defined
per-item over the artifact's declared class *"so it is a property of the artifact
and cannot be emptied by an engine — clause 3 applied to the ceiling as well as
to the gate."* The same two properties bind here: **the denominator is the
artifact's declared class, whole**, and the measure is **not a correctness
measure of the ordinary kind** but a count of self-descriptions that are
simultaneously answered, grounded and tagged, as §2.1's rows define those words.
`F` is still computed and reported beside it, ungated, because a ceiling that
hid the capped engine's fidelity would be quoting the flattering half — `R5`
clause 4's discipline, one layer on.

**Neither breached nor vacuous.** A ceiling binds only where the capped engine's
measured score is shown to be both, in the form `R7` clause 1 used for capped
`AUROC` at 500 against 600 and `README-l5 §4` demanded in advance. A Layer-8
`ATTAINABILITY.md` that reported *"0 ≤ ceiling, ceiling holds"* without reading
the headroom would have shipped a sentence that is true of a ceiling measuring
nothing; `R8` clause 7 read Layer 7's headroom as **eight items** and the same
reading is owed here.

### 4.2 What impossibility means at Layer 8, and why no fifth kind is minted here

`packaging/CATALOG.md §3` records the four-kind impossibility taxonomy as **final
below this document** — absent bits (L4), absent machinery (L5), absent order
(L6), absent generativity (L7) — and says so *"rather than claiming to be final
full stop"*, on the ground that Layer 8's humility side has nowhere obvious to
stand. This document does **not** mint the fifth kind, and the refusal is a rule
rather than a modesty:

> **A kind is named at its layer's own Stage B, from measurement, by the document
> that measures it.** That is the `l4`–`l7` precedent without exception: each
> kind was named by the `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` that had already run the capped engine
> and had both prongs in hand. A kind named here would be named by a document
> with no artifact, no capped measurement and no engine — the `IMPOSSIBILITY.md`
> equivalent of the number §0 refuses to invent.

What this document *does* fix is the **form** such an argument must take, which
is the taxonomy extended without being enlarged:

1. **It says which kind it is.** *"An `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` that could be any of the
   four is a template; one that says which it is has done the work"*
   (`CATALOG.md §3`). A Layer-8 argument states that it is one of the four **or**
   that it is a fifth.
2. **A fifth kind carries the closure properties the fourth had to carry.** It
   must **survive a perfect state** — nothing evicted, nothing damaged, nothing
   refused, and the number does not move — and be **closed under adding
   readings** and not only under adding budget, since a reading is a function of
   what it reads and its range is bounded by that. A candidate that fails either
   is one of the four wearing a new word.
3. **It is measured with both prongs.** `R5` clause 4's discipline: the flattering
   condition and the unflattering one, both, because the Layer-5 document's first
   draft was refuted by its own mandatory measurement and the corrected form was
   sharper.
4. **It says what would falsify it.** The `humility/l5` precedent, which also
   records which argument it is **not** making and why — the discipline that keeps
   `§6`'s obligation from degenerating into a template.

**And the axis the four kinds do not obviously cover is named, as an open axis
and not as a kind.** The first three ask *what is missing from the state?* — the
bits are gone, no verb reaches them, nothing orders them. The fourth asks *what
is missing from the closure of the operations?* A capped-7 engine at Layer 8 may
hold every bit, have every verb, order every answer and compose every item, and
still have **no term in its state that denotes its own state**. Whether that is
absent machinery in the sense `l5` got the word for, absent generativity in the
sense `l7` did, or something the ladder has not met, is a question a
**measurement** settles and this document does not. It is written down so that a
Stage-B session either fills it or refutes it, rather than rediscovering it.

**Layer 9's humility side, one sentence, same discipline.** A capped-8 engine has
a self-model and what it lacks is **emission**; the ceiling measure is §4.1's
(a count of correct emissions over the artifact's declared successor class,
never `F`), the kind is named at Layer 9's Stage B from measurement, and nothing
about it is settled here beyond the form.

## §5. The law seams

Four seams reach Layers 8 and 9. For each: **what binds from existing law
regardless**, **what this document adds**, and **what waits for a named ruling**.
Two are settled here; two are reserved, and reserved to a ruling this document
schedules rather than to a session's discretion.

### 5.1 `§4.2` for support that is a fact about state — the fourth seam

**What binds regardless.** `§4.2` binds from Layer 7 and **can never be
un-bound**. Every non-abstaining answer carries a valid tag or scores **0
regardless of whether its value is correct** (`§4.2.2`). `§4.2.3` fixes
`kind ∈ {recall, aggregate, derive, absent}` *"and no other"*, requires `support`
strictly ascending, non-negative and each an **actually-ingested** `t`, and
admits an **empty `support` only when `kind == "absent"`**. `R8` clause 5(a)
settled the reading **shape-only** — a support entry must be **ingested**, not
**recoverable** — with the weaker claim said out loud and paid for by an ungated
support-recoverability rate; clause 5(b) binds **relevance** on the artifact
because the schema cannot; clause 5(c) rules the schema **blind to lineage**.

**The measured seam this lands on.** `[L7] [PULSE]` recorded it as the fourth
seam of `README-l7 §5` (`BOUNDARY.log` line 48, `FIELD.md` 2026-08-03): **six
`query` ops answer non-abstaining with `provenance: null`** — `consolidation`,
`count`, `profile` (L4), `prospection` (L5), `calibration` (L6) and `lineage`
(L7), the layers' own diagnostics — while `forgetting` carries a valid `absent`
tag beside them. It is an **uneven** seam and not a blanket property of `query`,
no `§5 L7` denominator contains a diagnostic query so **no gated number moves**,
and the cause is in the engine's own source: a fold over the whole stream has no
bounded support to cite, and `§4.2.3` offers exactly **one bearer** for *"no
bounded support"* — the `absent` kind's empty `support` — which `forgetting`
takes and the other six decline to.

**What this document adds.** Three things, none of them a number and none of them
the choice itself.

* **The seam is now the layer's own subject.** At Layer 8 the untagged class and
  `§5 L8`'s subject are the **same class**: these six ops *are* introspection
  answered from state through the ordinary query interface. A Layer-8 gate is
  therefore stated over exactly the answers this seam is about, and cannot route
  around it.
* **The three candidate readings are stated exhaustively, so that a session
  cannot choose one by implementing it.**
  **(A) `absent` is the single bearer** and an unbounded fold answers with
  `kind = "absent"` and empty `support`. This violates no letter of `§4.2.3` —
  its constraint is on `support` — but `§4.2.3` glosses `absent` as *"justified
  negative / abstention-as-answer"*, and a fold's answer is neither, so this
  reading must **argue** that identification and not assume it.
  **(B) The support is bounded after all**: a self-descriptive answer cites the
  `t`s the **derivation procedure reads** (§3), which for most questions is a
  bounded set, and the seam then shrinks to the genuinely unbounded folds rather
  than covering the class. This is `R8` clause 5(b)'s move — relevance bound on
  the artifact — applied to `support` itself.
  **(C) Some self-descriptive answers cannot lawfully be answered under `§4.2`
  at all and must ABSTAIN.** `§7.3` makes that a scored abstention rather than a
  failure, and this outcome is **real and possibly correct**: it would make
  `§5 L8`'s capability narrower than it looks, and narrower is a finding.
* **One thing is ruled here, because it costs no number and follows from
  `§4.2.2` alone**: *no Layer-8 gate may be cleared by an engine whose
  self-descriptive answers are untagged.* `§4.2.2` has **no exception for a
  diagnostic**, this document declines to invent one, and an engine that answered
  the whole class untagged would be scoring 0 per answer under the law that binds
  it — so a gate cleared that way would be a gate read against the constitution.

**What waits for the ruling.** Which of (A), (B) and (C) binds — reserved to
**Layer 8's Stage A ruling**, in the `R4` / `R6` / `R7` / `R8` form (a reading
plus a substrate). It is reserved rather than taken because the honest options
are (A) an argument about what `absent` means, (B) a property of an artifact
nobody has built, and (C) a narrowing of a ratified capability — and because the
two ways to *fix* it in code are both forbidden: editing a frozen layer (`§9.2`)
or minting a schema form the frozen `§4.2.3` does not have, which is **an
amendment wearing a fix's clothes** (`README-l7 §5`'s own words).

### 5.2 Introspection is a `query` op — SETTLED

**What binds regardless.** `§7.1` declares **three** operations and `§1.1` says
events are the only fuel. `R6` clause 2 ratified the same argument for `intend`
and for `§7.1`'s *"appends one event"*; `R8` clause 2 ratified it for
`generate(cue)`; `README-l7 §5` derives it a fourth time for introspection from
ratified text alone. `trials/adapters/INTERFACE.md` copies `§7` verbatim and is
the contract every foreign engine is graded through.

**What this document adds — and it ratifies rather than deferring, because the
post-L7 `PULSE` asked for exactly that.**

> **Introspection is a `query` op.** Whatever Layer 8 asks, it asks through
> `query(state, q) -> answer`, and the Answer is `§7.2`'s Answer with its
> `confidence` and its `provenance`. **Layer 9's emission is a `query` op by the
> same argument**: a successor is not fuel, `§7.1` declares three doors, and an
> engine that grew a fourth verb would make `INTERFACE.md` false.

Three consequences follow from `§2.1`'s purity and are stated so a Stage C does
not have to discover them:

* **`query` is pure, so a self-description cannot record that it was asked.** An
  engine that wants to remember anything about itself must have it written by
  `ingest` — which is exactly what Layer 7 found when `promotion = 0` forced the
  lineage ledger onto the ingest path *"because `§7.1` makes `query` pure"*
  (`STAGE-B.md §5.1`, `BOUNDARY.log` line 45).
* **A self-description is a function of state at a `t`**, so `t_asof` is
  well-defined for it and `§4.2.3`'s `t_asof ≥ 0` is satisfiable — which is the
  one part of the fourth seam that is *not* in doubt.
* **`§7.3` governs the class the engine cannot answer**: an unsupported
  introspective op **abstains and is scored**, which is what gives §4 its
  humility construction and what makes a capped engine's behaviour on Layer 8's
  own tasks well-defined without any special harness convention.

### 5.3 `§5 L7`'s `promotion = 0` reaching an engine's own self-description

**What binds regardless.** `§5 L7`'s self-pollution clause — *"after re-ingesting
its own generations three deep, the engine must never promote generated-lineage
content to observed fact"* — is a Layer-7 gate clause, and the **inheritance**
class replays a lower layer's ratified gate at every higher cap, so a Layer-8
engine answers for it on `corpora/l7compose` exactly as a Layer-7 engine does.
`R8` clause 4 rules `generated` to be **item-lineage**, orthogonal to `§4.2.3`'s
closed answer-channel kinds, and places the marker in **engine state keyed by
`t`** (never in the payload, `§1.4`). `R8` clause 5(c) rules `promotion = 0`
enforced by the battery and by `§6`'s mandatory strain, **never** by
`laws/t_provenance_schema.py`.

**What this document adds.** The referent problem, named, with the constraint it
forces.

* **A self-model is composed content.** It is neither `recall` (no stored event
  carries it) nor exactly `derive` (it is not regenerated from stored content) —
  it is composed *about* the store. So the lineage question reaches it, it
  reaches it **per answer and not per op**, and `R8` clause 4's rule decides it
  unchanged: lineage is a property of the **item**, `kind` says how the answer
  reached the caller.
* **`§5 L9` makes the question unavoidable rather than academic.** *"Emit a
  functioning L1 successor from the self-model alone"* means the self-model is
  the material the emission composes from — and if the caller then re-ingests any
  of it, an engine that treated its own self-model as **observed fact** would be
  committing the capital crime **against itself**, at the one layer whose whole
  subject is what it knows about itself.
* **The novelty horizon's referent problem applies, and it forbids one clause
  shape.** `CATALOG.md §2` and `README-l7 §2.2` record that novelty is a
  canonical-byte comparison **against the ingested store**, so its referent
  **moves** once a generation is written back — measured at 60 → 30 → 0 across
  three rungs while `tagging` held at 1000 and `promotion` stayed 0. At Layer 8
  the problem is worse and is present from the first answer, not from the first
  re-ingestion: **a self-description is *about* the store, so its bytes may
  lawfully coincide with content the store holds**, and a correct
  self-description that happened to restate a stored payload would be scored
  un-novel for being right. Therefore: **a Layer-8 clause may not cite novelty
  in `§5 L7`'s byte-comparison-against-the-store form**, and any clause of that
  family must state its referent explicitly and defend it. This is a constraint
  on clause **shape**, not a threshold.

**What waits for the ruling.** Whether a self-description is `generated` for
promotion purposes — and hence whether a Layer-9 emission is itself a promotion
event and what its ladder's rungs are — is reserved to **Layer 8's Stage A ruling
for the self-description half** and to **Layer 9's for the emission half**, each
settled **before** its own gate binds, because `[L6] [DOGFOOD]`'s refusal to arm
a reminder about `R7` clause 7's bequest established that a question a Stage A
must decide is decided at that Stage A and not later.

### 5.4 `§3.0`'s price list over self-descriptive queries

**What binds regardless.** `§3.0`'s five rows, unamended. `R8` clause 8(a)
settled the Layer-7 interaction and **did not amend the price list**: it reads
**1000** correct-and-tagged / **100** abstain / **0** wrong / **0**
correct-but-untagged / **0** fabricate. `R7` clause 8's reserve **commitment
clause** — scoring an abstention 0 on a declared commitment class — remains
**declined and held in reserve**, its four objections standing.

**What this document adds.** The arithmetic that makes one clause shape unusable,
and a constant that is inherited rather than borrowed.

* **Abstention is cheap exactly where self-description is hardest.** `§7.3`
  guarantees an engine with no self-model a **scored abstention** on every
  introspective query — 100 apiece — so an engine that describes nothing is not
  at zero, it is at the abstention floor. `F` therefore cannot carry `R2`
  obligation 2 at this layer.
* **The hedger's window is the same constant, derived and not borrowed.** A
  policy abstaining on a hard class of share `g` scores `F = 1000 − 900g`, so an
  `F ≥ 950` clause survives blanket abstention on that class iff `g ≤ 50/900 =
  1/18`. That is `R7` clause 3(c)'s `A < 18r` and `R8` clause 3(e)'s ladder, and
  it recurs at **any** layer whose fidelity clause is `≥ 950` over an
  all-answerable hard class. `R7` clause 3(c)'s **lower** bound (`A ≥ 10r`) is
  expressly **not** inherited: it came from a forced error under a withheld coin,
  and whether Layer 8 withholds anything from a correct describer is exactly the
  open question §3 leaves to Stage A.
* **So the binding constraint is on the artifact and is stated as a
  precondition**, in `R8` clause 8(b)'s form: **a Layer-8 gate binds only on an
  artifact whose hard class is a large enough share of its answerable core that
  blanket abstention breaks the fidelity clause** — the escape priced out
  **before** the gate binds rather than legislated against afterwards. The share
  is arithmetic and its value is deferred with everything else.
* **And where no sizing satisfies every ratified clause at once, the session
  stops.** That is `R7` clause 3(c)'s own rule — an `ATTEMPT`-shaped
  constitutional finding, logged, with the engine withheld — and it is restated
  here because Layer 8 is likelier to meet it than any layer below.

**What waits for the ruling.** Whether `R7` clause 8's commitment clause is
called for at Layer 8 is **reserved**, and this document expressly does **not**
call for it: its four objections are unanswered because none has needed
answering, and the instrument that has done the work at Layers 6 and 7 — a
sizing that kills the hedger on a ratified clause — is the one §5.4 names above.

## §8. Where this document is checked

`trials/laws/t_boundary_high.py`, in the `laws/` class rather than in `ops/`
because what it guards is **legality** and not capability (`§6`): a threshold
applied without authority is an illegal threshold whatever any engine scores
against it, and a canon-grade document that stopped being append-only would
break the discipline every layer above it will rest on. Four things are checked:

1. **the document exists and is well-formed** — its status block, its
   subordination to `BOUNDARY.md`, its append-only declaration, and the sections
   this schedule refers to;
2. **the committed hash is pinned** — a `sha256` recorded in the trial, so a
   change to this file is red unless the same commit changes the pin, which is
   what makes an amendment visible and a ruling's job;
3. **the append-only property holds against git history** — every committed
   version is a byte-exact prefix of the file on disk, the same walk
   `t_rulings.py` runs over `BOUNDARY.log` and `BOUNDARY-RULINGS.md`. It is
   vacuous exactly once, on the commit that creates the file, exactly as
   `BOUNDARY-RULINGS.md`'s was;
4. **the no-number property** — every `threshold` cell reads
   `DEFERRED — Stage A + ruling` and carries no digit; no Layer-8 or Layer-9
   trial directory, engine file or adapter exists; and no gate constant anywhere
   under `trials/` cites this document as its authority.

The fourth is the one with the mutation behind it. A session that wrote a number
into a threshold cell here, or a `GATE_*` constant into a Layer-8 trial, would be
turning a deferral into an authority in a diff that changes one token. **It goes
red instead.**
