# Layer 6 — Meta-memory (confidence permille, from structural evidence)

`[L6] [ASCEND]`. The sixth capability of Boundary-1: Memory. Layer 1 **retained**,
Layer 2 **recalled**, Layer 3 **dropped**, Layer 4 **derived**, Layer 5
**watched**. Every one of those is a claim about the world, and every one of them
this engine has been able to *prove* — an event is regenerated byte-exact or it
is not, a fold inverts or its key is refused, an intention has fired or it has
not. `README-l5 §4` wrote that down as the seam this layer opens on:

> *"There is no quantity in this state that varies with how likely an answer is
> to be right, because every answer it gives is one it has proved."*

Layer 6 is where that stops being true, and it is the first layer in this
repository whose engine is **wrong on purpose** — wrong exactly where a theorem
forces it, with a confidence that says so beforehand.

This document states **exactly what Layer 6 can and cannot express**. Layer 7's
humility trial is written against the boundary drawn in §4.

Intellectual pedigree: `autopsy/theory-actr-soar/BRIEF.md §5` records ACT-R's
activation→retrieval-probability logistic as this layer's ancestor, *"calibrated
to trial truth, not fit to human data"* — and the fit here is not a logistic and
not a fit: it is an exact rational the construction states about itself.
`autopsy/GAPMAP.md §1` finds calibration **ABSENT** in all four engines
autopsied and in every evaluator; there is no prior art to steal from, and what
is inherited is `§3.4`'s own arithmetic and Layer 4's discipline.

Code (frozen after this session, §9):
- `core/layers/l6_meta_memory.py` — the `L6State` (a **subclass** of the frozen
  `L5State` that adds no field), the declared set-once reading, the evidence
  folds, `§3.5`'s permille, and the confidence the Answer carries.
- `trials/adapters/l6.py` — the Layer-6 adapter. `core/engine.py`,
  `l1_retention.py`, `l2_recall.py`, `l3_forgetting.py`, `l4_consolidation.py`
  and `l5_prospection.py` are **untouched**; `anchors/l1.json` … `anchors/l5.json`
  replay through their own adapters unchanged.

## §0. The budget decided nothing, and that is the finding

Every ASCEND since Layer 3 has opened with a state-composition arithmetic: `§0`
of `README-l3`, `README-l4` and `README-l5` each spent a recorded margin and each
reported where it ran out — 14 items, 5 cells, 10 cells. This one has no such
paragraph, because the constitution wrote the answer down first. `§5.1 L6`,
defending `B = 1000`:

> *Meta-memory derives confidence **from existing state** within budget.*

Taken literally, that is a claim about the shape of the layer and not a
reassurance about its size, and it is taken literally here. **`L6State` adds no
field to the frozen `L5State`.** `_body` is `l5._body` unchanged, so the
canonical state of a Layer-6 engine over any stream is identical to the Layer-5
engine's over the same stream in **every branch but one** — the recorded
`layer_cap`, which is the cap and not the content. `anchors/l6.json` pins that as
an identity against `anchors/l5.json`'s own frozen figures, branch by branch and
field by field, so a Layer-6 engine that grew a structure goes red against a file
this session could not edit (§9.2).

### 0.1 The 18 cells, and where they went

`ATTAINABILITY-B.md §3.2` priced the marginal state at **18 cells** — one
set-once flag per attribute key across battery-b's 18-key vocabulary, *"priced as
state because that is where it would live"* — and disclaimed the loss reserve
with its reason. The engine spends **0**, and under `R6` clause 3 the document
stands as written while the divergence is recorded here:

| item | priced | spent | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `set_once` per attribute key | 18 | **0** | it is a **declared reading of the frozen grammars**, in the shape `ASSERTION_FORMS` (L4) and `INTENTION_FORM` (L5) already had — not a fold over the stream, so there is nothing to materialize |
| `n_assert`, `n_distinct`, `verbatim_repeats`, `assert_span` | 0 | **0** | read off the interval table the Layer-4 engine already holds |
| loss-accounting reserve | disclaimed | — | scored in budget, where nothing is evicted; §3 below is what the disclaimer left open |

`R5` clause 4 forbids an **unpriced** item, not a cheaper one: *"an unpriced item
is not a saving; it is a margin that has already been spent."* Nothing here is
unpriced. What the 18 cells were is a ceiling on where the reading could have
lived, and the reading lives one step earlier than the ceiling assumed.

The consequence is worth stating in its own right, because it is what
`inheritance/l6`'s Layer-5 row rests on: a confidence model that cost cells would
compete for room with a pending set and a fired ledger that `README-l5 §0.1` puts
outside every eviction phase **on purpose**, and the cheapest way to buy those
cells would be a lossier derived view that no `§5 L6` clause would ever notice.
One that costs none cannot. The Layer-5 row is not merely green; it is green for
a structural reason.

## §1. The confidence, in four lines

### 1.1 A confidence model, not a second reader

`§5 L6` asks for *"confidence permille from structural evidence"*. It does not
ask for better answers, and on the artifact `R7` clause 1 binds it could not: the
forcing region is exactly the class **no reader can get right** (`R7` clause
3(b), Theorem 1), so a battery that rewarded a better answer there would be
rewarding an engine that had read the answer key.

So the answer path is the frozen Layer-4 one, byte for byte. `current` is still
the value at a chain's greatest `t`; `asof` is still the value in force at the
asked `t`, honouring supersession and abstaining before a pair's first assertion.
`strain/l6::trial_the_engine_is_wrong_only_where_the_theorem_forces_it` asserts
that as an identity over all 2 400 queries of the binding artifact — the Layer-6
engine's `status` and `value` equal the frozen Layer-5 engine's, query by query.
**Layer 6 attaches a number. It does not answer differently.**

### 1.2 The evidence: two folds and a reading

| feature | where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `set_once(key)` | `SET_ONCE_KEYS`, a **declared reading of the frozen grammars** |
| `n_distinct(chain)` | the `(entity, key)` chain visible to the query, by **canonical bytes** (§2.4) |

`origin` is the declared set-once key. It is `corpora/murk`'s set-once attribute
in that generator's own frozen text — *"re-assert an entity's set-once attribute
`origin` with a DIFFERENT value (a genuine contradiction, not a legal update)"* —
and it is `corpora/l6batteryb`'s forcing key, asserted there by nothing but the
region.

**It is a reading because it cannot be anything else**, and saying so is half the
honesty of this layer. Nothing in a stream distinguishes a set-once key from an
ordinary key that happened to be updated: a chain disagreeing with itself is a
*contradiction* only where the key admits one value, and which keys those are is
a fact about the grammar, not about the bytes. An engine that guessed would price
legal updates as contradictions — which is precisely what the **key-blind**
`conflict-rank` policy does, and `ATTAINABILITY-B.md §4.3` measures what it costs:
it clears `AUROC ≥ 900` on 11 permille where the set-once-aware witness clears it
on 76.

`n_distinct` counts **canonical bytes** and not assertions, for `README-l4 §1`'s
reason (`True == 1` in Python and `true ≠ 1` here) and for one of its own: a value
asserted twice is *one* claimant asserted twice. That is the difference between
`corpora/murk`'s near-duplicate family and its contradiction family, and it is
asserted directly — a verbatim repeat injected into a watched chain must **not**
move the confidence, while a distinct value must.

### 1.3 The tie rate: `permille(1/d)`, derived and not chosen

Where a set-once key's visible chain carries `d > 1` distinct values, the chain
holds `d` mutually exclusive claimants for a slot that admits one, the engine's
reading commits to one of them, and

```
confidence = permille(1 / d)          exact Fraction in, integer permille out
```

At `d = 2` that is **500** — the number `ATTAINABILITY-B.md §3.1`'s exhibited
witness prices the forcing region at, and prices it as `permille(1/2)`, *derived
and not chosen*. The engine states the same number for the same reason, and the
reason generalises past the artifact: `ops/l6/t_l6_composition.py` exhibits the
ladder at `d = 2, 3, 4, 5` on a fixture, because a level nothing exercises is a
level that could be wrong.

Everywhere else the engine states **1000**, because everywhere else its answer is
one it has proved. `read`, `read_range`, `recall`, `fired`, `profile`, `count`
and the diagnostics are unchanged from Layer 5: they return content the engine
regenerated exactly, or they abstain.

### 1.4 The horizon is the prefix

`asof` prices the evidence the answer actually **had**: the chain up to the asked
`t`, which is the same prefix `_l6btasks.evidence` takes. The consequence is
visible and pinned — an `as-of` asked before a set-once key's second,
contradicting assertion sees one value, is right, and says 1000, while `current`
on the same chain sees two, is a coin flip, and says 500. A confidence that
ignored the horizon would understate the first.

### 1.5 Provably non-resolving

The model reads a chain's **shape** and never the raw entity id or an absolute
`t` — the two handles `R7` clause 3(b)'s Theorem 1 leaves, and the two the
declared evidence vocabulary excludes *"because including them would let a
class-E policy split a pair"*. So on the two members of a mirror pair it computes
the identical confidence and commits to the identical reading, which is exactly
why it is wrong on one of them.

`ops/l6` asserts the **cause** (rebuild a tie under four different entity ids at
four different logical times: the number does not move) and `ascension/l6` and
`strain/l6` assert the **consequence** (no pair is resolved). An engine right on
both members of a pair would have read a coin that regenerating the artifact
under its complement proves is not in the stream (Theorem 2) — class **O** by
definition, and the one check in the battery that catches an engine for being
*too good*.

## §2. What the strains found (`trials/strain/l6/`)

### 2.1 Bias: the evidence BINDS the confidence

`autopsy/GAPMAP.md §6` assigns **bias** to consolidation and the post-L4 PULSE
(`BOUNDARY.log` line 24) recorded it half-covered, with the other half deferred in
as many words: *"the catalog's own clause is about stated CONFIDENCE and stays
L6's, calibration being dormant per §3.4"*. The post-L5 PULSE carried the
reservation forward unchanged. It is discharged here, and it had to be discharged
as a **binding** rather than as a display, because `autopsy/GAPMAP.md §2`'s engine
thesis is that every system autopsied writes the metadata that would make it
correct and then never reads it where it counts. A confidence that does not move
when the evidence moves is that thesis landing on the one field this layer exists
to fill — `mem0`'s silently coexisting contradictions wearing calibration's
clothes.

Measured on a **watched chain of the frozen forcing region**, injected through
`ingest` like any other event and read back through `current`:

| the chain now holds | distinct claimants | the engine states |
|---|---|---|
| the frozen pair `(x, y)` | 2 | **500** |
| a verbatim repeat of `y` | 2 | **500** — unmoved |
| a third value | 3 | **333** |
| a fourth | 4 | **250** |
| a fifth | 5 | **200** |

Both directions at once, and neither tradeable for the other. The teeth are
**demonstrated rather than assumed**, in the shape `strain/l3` established for
its inflation guard and `strain/l5` for its ledger-blind policy: a
constant-confidence reference policy — a trial fixture, never engine code — sees
the identical stream and states 1000 throughout.

### 2.2 No manufactured errors

`AUROC` needs a non-empty error class and an engine that wanted one could
manufacture it. `R7` clause 3(b) puts that burden on the **artifact**; this is
the converse asserted of the **engine**, and asserted the strongest way
available — not that the error count is small, but that the whole answer surface
is the frozen Layer-5 engine's. On the clean core (`K2` 1 400 current-value,
`K3` 600 as-of) both engines are wrong **0** times; on the region the engine is
wrong exactly **100**, one member of every mirror pair, which is Theorem 1's
floor and ceiling at once and the same 100 all six readers on
`t_attainability_b.py`'s bench measure.

An engine that sandbagged the clean core to buy itself an error class would go
red there while every `§5 L6` clause stayed green — a trial that catches a gate
being *gamed* rather than failed.

### 2.3 The denominator law under adversarial abstention

`autopsy/GAPMAP.md §2`'s second thesis convicted every evaluator read of retiring
abstention from scoring, and `R7` clause 3(a) is grounded on the sharpest case:
WRIT drops a declared-absent capability from **both** numerator and denominator
(`evaluator.ts:545-548`). This trial is that thesis inverted. Four abstention
patterns are put through the shared instrument — none, all, the tie, and a
fixture that consults the **answer key** to hedge exactly what it got right — and
for every one of them `§3.4`'s `A` is exactly the answered queries, `A` splits
exactly into `n_pos + n_neg`, `A + abstentions = N`, and the per-class
declaration rebuilds `A`. The fourth pattern is not an engine and could not be; it
is there because a law about a denominator is only a law if it holds against a
pattern chosen to break it.

Two results are recorded rather than left to be rediscovered:

* **the hedger is killed by `F`, not by the region trial.** An engine wearing
  `detect-and-abstain` takes `n_neg` to 0 and `AUROC` to `n/a` — which `R7`
  clause 3(a) rules disqualifies — *and* falls to `F 101/110 → 918` against 950,
  which is `ATTAINABILITY-B.md §5`'s `k = 100` ladder row reproduced on an engine.
  The region trial is correctly **silent** against it, because hedging the whole
  region leaves nothing committed. `STAGE-B.md §5.1` found the same thing on the
  mock; this is it on the engine.
* **`F` over the answerable core cannot see a fabrication at all.** Its
  denominator is the answerable core — the one every piece of `R7` clause 3(c)'s
  window arithmetic is stated in — and a fabrication happens on an *unanswerable*
  query, outside it. A fixture answering all 200 absence probes still measures
  `F_core 955` and clears the fidelity clause. What catches it is the pair the
  battery already carries and neither alone: `fabricated = 0`, and `F_all < F_core`,
  which the ascension battery states in exactly those words. **This trial's first
  draft asserted the wrong one and was corrected rather than relaxed**; the
  stricter denominator is stricter about the region and blind about the probes,
  which is why the looser one is computed and reported beside it rather than
  discarded.

### 2.4 The evidence under pressure

The fourth strain is §4's open item, measured rather than predicted, and it found
the prediction wrong in the direction that costs nothing and the direction that
does not. It is stated on a fixture and **asserts its own scope**: neither
Layer-6 artifact is scored under any pressure at all — `refused = 0`,
`forgotten = 0`, no entity damaged — so nothing here moves a gated number, and a
later ruling that bound a footprint clause at this layer would reach it on the
first write.

## §3. What Layer 6 CAN express

- **Calibration at the ratified gate.** On the binding artifact
  (`corpora/l6batteryb`, `BOUNDARY-RULINGS.md R7` clause 1), read **exact** under
  clause 4 and binned under clause 5:

  ```
  Brier 1/44 -> 23      ECE 0 exactly      AUROC 41/42 -> 976
  F 21/22 -> 955 (answerable core; 23/24 -> 958 over the whole set, ungated)
  B 1000    A 2200    n_pos 2100    n_neg 100    refused 0    fabricated 0
  ```

  against a gate of `≤ 40 / ≤ 30 / ≥ 900 / ≥ 950 / = 1000`. The engine's figures
  are the exhibited witness's, clause for clause — which is the point of `R2`
  obligation 1 and not a coincidence: `ATTAINABILITY-B.md` exhibited a confidence
  assignment before any engine existed, and Stage C's burden was to compute it
  **from the engine's own state** rather than from the battery's declared
  evidence.

- **Committing where hedging would pay.** `§3.0` prices an abstention on an
  answerable query at 100 against a confident error's 0, so the engine is offered
  90 permille of fidelity to walk away from `§3.4`'s denominator. It takes none:
  its 200 abstentions are exactly the 200 unanswerable probes, its denominator is
  exactly the 2 200-query answerable core, and it fabricates nothing.

- **Being wrong, with the number stated first.** 100 errors, all of them one
  member of a mirror pair, every one carrying `confidence = 500` — the region's
  own measured accuracy of exactly one half. That is what makes `ECE = 0`
  **exactly** rather than nearly: bin 5 carries 200 answers at a mean confidence
  of one half against an accuracy of one half, and bin 9 carries 2 000 at 1000
  against 1, so the partition agrees with itself under `R7` clause 5's bin index.

- **A confidence that moves with the evidence**, downward with each further
  distinct claimant on a set-once chain and not at all with a verbatim repeat —
  and *not at all* on a key the reading does not call set-once, where the latest
  assertion is what is in force and the engine is right to say so.

- **Layers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5, carried forward.** `inheritance/l6` replays §5 L1's
  exact IO and byte-identical round trip, §5 L2's cue gate, §5 L3's retention
  battery on both frozen pressure streams, §5 L4's consolidation battery on
  `corpora/l4stream`, and §5 L5's prospection battery on `corpora/l5stream` — the
  last as identities (`precision = recall = 1000`, `dup-fire = miss = 0`,
  `F = 1000`), because intentions must still fire exactly once under a cap-6
  engine and nothing in `ascension/l6` scores a firing.

- **The demoted diagnostic's cause, on an engine.** `corpora/l6battery` is
  replayed, scored and reported **ungated** (`R7` clause 1), and there the same
  model measures `Brier 8 / ECE 18 / AUROC 1000 / F 955` over `A 3550`,
  `n_neg 158`. **AUROC 1000** — the ceiling — because on murk the ties the engine
  reads are *exactly* its errors: `§8.7` injects every defect by visible
  construction, so evidence that ranks also resolves. A gate bound there would
  have been measuring an artifact's transparency rather than a model. The two
  numbers side by side, anchored in `anchors/l6.json`, are what the fourth
  substrate kill was for.

## §4. What Layer 6 CANNOT express (the boundary for Layer 7+)

- **No generation, and — the seam that matters — NO PROVENANCE BINDING YET.**
  `§4.2` is **dormant until Layer 7 and binding forever after**. Tags are
  attached and are derivable today (a `current` answer cites the assertion that
  carries it, `kind: "derive"`), but they are neither required nor scored, and
  **nothing this engine returns is tagged `generated` because nothing this engine
  returns is generated.** A capped-6 engine tags no generation — it has no
  `generate` verb, no novelty construct and no lineage field — and `§5 L7` makes
  that the constitution's named capital crime: `tagging = 1000`, and *"an
  untagged generation is a fabrication"*.

  Stated for the layer above, as `README-l5 §4` stated this one's seam: **a
  capped-6 engine should fail `§5 L7` structurally rather than marginally.**
  `§5 L7` gates `validity = 1000`, `novelty = 1000`, `tagging = 1000`,
  self-pollution promotion `= 0` three deep, `F ≥ 950`, `B = 1000` and
  `ECE ≤ 40`, with a humility ceiling of capped `(novel ∧ valid ∧ tagged) ≤ 50`.
  Make it exact against this engine: the conjunction is **0**, not near 0, and by
  arithmetic rather than by margin — `novelty` requires an item *provably never
  stored*, and every value this engine can return is one it holds, so the novel
  conjunct is false on every answer it has and the other two conjuncts cannot
  rescue a product with a zero in it. The ceiling of 50 is not approached from
  below by a poor generator; there is no generator.

  And one honest residual, which is where Layer 7 will have to do work this layer
  cannot do for it: `ECE ≤ 40` at Layer 7 is stated over answers that include
  **generated** ones, and this layer's whole evidence vocabulary is folds over an
  interval table of things that were **ingested**. A generated item has no chain,
  no distinct-value count and no set-once status, so `confidence_for` returns
  `CERTAIN` on it by falling through — which is exactly the wrong answer and is
  the first thing a Layer-7 engine must replace. It is not a defect at Layer 6
  (there is nothing to generate) and it is recorded here so that the Layer-7
  session finds it rather than discovers it.

- **No confidence on anything but an assertion chain.** `read`, `read_range`,
  `recall`, `fired`, `profile` and `count` all return `1000`, unchanged from
  Layer 5, because they return content the engine regenerated exactly or they
  abstain. That is honest today and it is a **non-capability by construction**:
  there is no quantity in this state that varies with how likely a *reconstruction*
  is to be right, because a reconstruction that could be wrong is one this engine
  refuses to make (`README-l4 §1`, *fold only what inverts*).

- **Confidence under eviction — the item `R7` left open, ANSWERED, and the
  prediction it was left with was wrong.** `ATTAINABILITY-B.md §3.2` disclaimed
  the loss reserve because the artifacts are scored **in budget**, and named the
  failure it feared in advance: *"a model reading a table that has forgotten the
  tie would be confident **at 1000 on a coin flip**, which is the worst failure
  this layer has."* `R7`'s *"what this ruling does not do"* left it to Stages B
  and C; `STAGE-B.md §7` recorded that no in-budget battery can measure an answer
  and passed it here. Stage C measured it before choosing, and the measurement
  replaced the prediction:

  > **The feared path does not exist.** `l4_consolidation._shed_until`'s unit is
  > the **whole `(key, entity)` chain and never part of one** — its own docstring
  > says why, because a partly-dropped entity could still be asked for a profile
  > and *"a fold over what survived would be a confident undercount"*. So a shed
  > set-once tie does not become a smaller tie: the pair stops being answerable
  > and the engine **abstains**, which `§3.0` pays 100 for and which states
  > nothing false. Shedding alone cannot make this engine confident on a coin
  > flip. `README-l4`'s summary of shedding as removing a chain *prefix* is
  > corrected by the code it describes, and under `R6` clause 3 the divergence is
  > recorded here rather than edited there.
  >
  > **The path that does exist is one step later, and no document had named
  > it.** A set-once key **re-asserted after its chain was shed** rebuilds a chain
  > of length one. A model counting only what it can see reads `d = 1`, calls that
  > certainty, and states **1000** on a value whose true rival is already in the
  > forgetting record. Measured, on a fixture, at a 90-cell cap.
  >
  > **The answer, and it costs nothing.** `damaged` is Layer-4 state that already
  > means *this entity's history is incomplete*. `claimants` reads the same flag:
  > for a set-once key on a damaged entity, at least one claimant is provably
  > unrecoverable, so the count is `d + 1` and the stated confidence becomes an
  > **upper bound on the engine's own correctness** rather than a census of the
  > survivors. `+1` and not more, because how many more is a question the
  > aggregated per-`t`-range record cannot answer — `humility/l4/IMPOSSIBILITY.md
  > §3`'s pigeonhole, one layer up. The re-asserted chain above therefore states
  > **500**, not 1000. An ordinary key on the same damaged entity does **not**
  > move, and that asymmetry is the whole content of the rule: only a set-once
  > slot can have its truth in the part of a chain the budget took.
  >
  > **Scope, stated rather than assumed.** None of this is reachable on either
  > Layer-6 artifact: both are scored at `DEFAULT_BUDGET` with `refused = 0`,
  > `forgotten = 0` and **no entity damaged**, which
  > `strain/l6::trial_the_shed_evidence_seam_is_unreachable_on_both_layer_6_artifacts`
  > asserts. No score in `§3` moves by a permille. What was closed is a behaviour,
  > not a number — and a later ruling that bound a footprint clause at this layer
  > would reach it on the first write.

- **No learned evidence.** `SET_ONCE_KEYS` is a declared reading of the frozen
  grammars, not a learner, and §1.2 records why it cannot be one. A future corpus
  whose set-once vocabulary differs would need the reading extended — which is a
  reading and therefore a session's declaration checked against the battery's, not
  something an engine may infer.

- **No cancellation of a stated confidence, no second reader, no re-answer.**
  Layer 6 attaches a number to the answer Layer 4 gives. It has no path by which a
  low confidence becomes an abstention, and that is deliberate: `§3.0` is
  **confidence-blind** — a calibrated hedge and a confident lie score identically
  there — so an engine that converted its own low confidence into an abstention
  would be following the incentive `R7` clause 7 records, emptying `§3.4`'s
  denominator, and dying at `F 918`. The engine commits. The number is what it has
  to say about committing.
