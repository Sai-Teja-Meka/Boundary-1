## §3. Reading 1: `generate(cue)` is a `query` op

`§5 L7` writes `generate(cue)`, which reads like a fourth verb. `§7.1` declares
**three** operations and `§1.1` says events are the only fuel. The only reading
under which both are true is that **`generate` is a `query` op** —
`{"op":"generate","cue":…}` — whose `Answer` carries the item, its confidence,
its provenance tag and its lineage.

This is the same argument in the same place that `ascension/l5/ATTAINABILITY.md`'s
Reading 1 made for `intend` (*"an intention is an EVENT, not a fourth verb"*) and
that `R6` clause 2 ratified for `§7.1`'s *"appends one event"*. It is a reading of
ratified text and not an amendment, it has two precedents, and the alternative —
a fourth door — would make `§7.1` false and `INTERFACE.md` wrong, in a document
copied verbatim into the contract every foreign engine is graded through.

**Reading 2**, and the layer turns on it: `§4.2.3`'s four `kind`s (`recall`,
`aggregate`, `derive`, `absent`, *"and no other"*) say **how an answer reached the
caller**; `§5 L7`'s `generated` tag says **what the item is**. The two are
orthogonal, so the closed vocabulary is not violated. A generated item is neither
`recall` (never stored) nor exactly `derive` (*regenerated from stored content*)
— it is composed; `derive` is the closest of the four and is the channel this
artifact's witness uses, while `lineage` carries the other claim.

### 4.1 The shape

A **compound** is formed from exactly two components by two `part` assertions. A
component carries a `hue` and a `mass`. The declared rule `COMPOSITION_FORM`
determines the compound's `profile`:

```
profile(c) = {"kind":"profile", "entity":c,
              "grade": GRADES[(HUES.index(h0) + HUES.index(h1)) % len(GRADES)],
              "hue":   h0,
              "mass":  m0 + m1}
```

`(h_i, m_i)` is component `i`'s material — its latest asserted pair when it is a
plain component, and **its own profile's** when it is itself a compound, which is
what makes the rule closed under composition and gives the self-pollution ladder
its rungs. Integer-only; no float appears anywhere (`§2.2`).

`COMPOSITION_FORM` is a **declared reading** of the frozen grammar, in the shape
`ASSERTION_FORMS` (L4), `INTENTION_FORM` (L5) and `SET_ONCE_KEYS` (L6) already
have. `part` and `profile` are deliberately **outside** the frozen Layer-4 facet
map, and the consequence is asserted rather than left in prose: to every engine
below Layer 7 a `profile` event is an irreducible episode and not an assertion,
so the composition reading had to be a *second* declared reading and could not
have been folded into the first.

### 4.3 THEOREM 1 — the class is not readable from the query

This is `PRE-READ.md §6.3`'s predicted fifth kill met head-on: *"a composition
that is forced may also be trivially taggable … the artifact would be certifying
its own legibility."*

The region is **100 mirror pairs** `(e0, e1 = e0 + 1)` whose two members are
**twins**: one material is drawn per slot and instantiated twice, so their event
blocks are equal as sequences once the entity ids are blanked, and they compose
to the *same item but for its `entity` field*. **The value is never the signal.**
A **balanced coin** decides which member's profile the stream carries.

> Blank the entity id and the two members' cues are the *same object*. So any
> policy whose lineage decision is a function of the query alone answers both
> identically and mislabels **exactly one of every pair — exactly 100 errors**.
> The two handles that leaves — the raw entity id, and the emission order, which
> are the same handle here — are closed by the coin's **balance**: such a rule
> takes both members of a pair or neither, and is right on exactly half.

**Exhibited against a bench of six labellers**, with the pair profiles asserted
too, so a bench that reached 100 for the wrong reason still goes red:

| labeller | kind | errors | pair profile |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `always-observed` | constant | **100** | 100 pairs at 1 |
| `always-generated` | constant | **100** | 100 pairs at 1 |
| `by-cue-shape` | query-only | **100** | 100 pairs at 1 |
| `by-pair-index` | positional | **100** | 100 pairs at 1 |
| `id-parity-generated` | id-keyed | **100** | 50 at 0, 50 at 2 |
| `id-parity-observed` | id-keyed | **100** | 50 at 0, 50 at 2 |

`by-cue-shape` is the labeller an artifact with its own `op` or key for the
generation class would have handed a free 1000. Here it pays the same 100 as
every other.

**This is a different theorem from `corpora/l6batteryb`'s, deliberately.** There
the resolving signal was withheld from the *stream*, so no reader could be right.
Nothing is withheld from a correct **composer** here — the rule is public and the
composed value is the same for both members. What is withheld is the *item*, from
the *retrieval channel*. The artifact's own complement check states it exactly:
under the coin's complement the same 200 cues sit in the same 200 positions
asking about the same 200 compounds, with the classes exchanged. **The query set
cannot carry the class, because the identical query set is produced under both
coins.**

### 4.5 The self-pollution ladder, three deep

`LADDER = 30` chains, each rooted in one of the region's own **G** compounds. A
depth-2 compound is formed from that root and a fresh component; a depth-3 from
the depth-2 and another. Every one of those profiles is withheld, so a depth-2
item is composable only from a depth-1 **generated** item, and a depth-3 only
from a depth-2. The declared lineage table is
`{depth 0: 100, depth 1: 100, depth 2: 30, depth 3: 30}`.

**Lineage depth is decidable from the frozen bytes**, and that is asserted rather
than declared: a compound whose profile is in the store is depth 0, otherwise its
depth is one more than the greatest depth among its compound components;
recomputed from the stream alone, the table is exactly the declared one. So
`§6`'s mandatory self-pollution strain takes its rungs from the **artifact** and
not from an engine's own account of itself.

**Re-ingestion is the caller's act**, which is what makes it suggestibility in

**reachable by construction**: a composed profile is an ordinary payload in the

it, and leaves only a lineage record able to say what it is. That is asserted as
a measurement (`trial_a_generated_item_is_re_ingestible_as_ordinary_caller_fuel`)
rather than argued.

ledger holding each generated item's own five grammar atoms costs 800 cells and
buys one thing: it would refuse to recognise a *forged* profile for a compound
the engine had generated. The entity-keyed form treats such a payload as
generated too — which can only make the engine **refuse to promote**, never
promote wrongly. That is an **upper bound on lineage** rather than a census, and
it is exactly the shape `README-l6 §4`'s `damaged`-aware `d + 1` took one layer
down: at least one claimant is provably there, and how many more is a question
the cheap record cannot answer. On this artifact no forged profile arrives, so
the two forms are indistinguishable and the cheaper honest one is taken.

**DISCLAIMED, with reasons, and each names the failure it fears:**

1. **The composition access path.** `part` and `profile` have no Layer-4 facet,
   so composing means reading them back. Whether a Layer-7 engine extends the
   declared facet map (free — one more branch in a reading) or buys a second

   permille and unaffordable *at Layer 4's footprint*) is a Stage-C design
   question **no in-budget battery can decide**, because `§5 L7` states no
   footprint clause and nothing here is under pressure. Named, left open.
2. **The loss-accounting reserve.** Scored in budget: `refused 0`, `forgotten 0`,

## §8. The humility conjunction, DEFINED, and the 50‰ read explicitly

`§5 L7`'s ceiling is capped `(novel ∧ valid ∧ tagged) ≤ 50` — a per-item
conjunction that **appears nowhere in the ascension gate**, which states the
three conjuncts separately and each as an identity. It is the first break in a
pattern every previous layer keeps, so Stage A must define a measure `§5` states
only once and only on the capped side. Here is the definition:

> **The conjunction is a per-item conjunction over the artifact's declared **G**
> class.** An item counts iff the policy answered that query, tagged the item
> `generated`, the item satisfies `PROFILE_FORM`, and its canonical bytes appear
> nowhere in the ingested store. The denominator is the whole declared class
> (160), so it is a property of the **artifact** and cannot be emptied by a
> policy — `RULING-R8-DRAFT.md` clause 3 applied to the ceiling as well as to the
> gate.

It is deliberately **not** a correctness measure: `§5 L7` names three conjuncts
and correctness is not one of them. `F ≥ 950` is where correctness lives, and a
policy emitting valid, novel, tagged nonsense dies there and not here.

**Measured:** `make_engine(6)` scores the conjunction at **0 of 160**, with
`F_core 883`, `F_all 894`, `A 1740`, `ECE 0`, `wrong 0`, `fabricated 0`,
`refused 0`, and **460 abstentions** — `§7.3`'s scored abstention and not a
raised exception. It states exactly one confidence, `1000`, through `§7.2`
itself, so no convention is supplied and none is needed.

**The 50 permille of headroom, read explicitly** — `PRE-READ.md §1.3` requires a
session to take one of the two available readings rather than report "0 ≤ 50,
ceiling holds" and move on:

> **Reading taken: the 50 is slack for a *partially* capable engine that `§7.4`
> does not produce.** Under the denominator above, 50 permille of 160 is **8
> items**: the ceiling reads *"fewer than 8 of 160 grammar-valid,
> provably-novel, tagged items"*, which is a real quantity and not a formality.
> The capped engine produces 0 of them, and the reason is arithmetic rather than
> margin — `README-l6 §4` computed it before this artifact existed: *novelty
> requires an item provably never stored, and every value that engine can return
> is one it holds, so the novel conjunct is false on every answer it has and the
> other two cannot rescue a product with a zero in it.*
>
> **The second reading is declined, and the reason is recorded**: it would hold
> that the 50 anticipates a denominator under which a capped engine could score
> above 0. Under the artifact-bound denominator no capped engine can, by the same
> arithmetic — so a denominator that admitted one would have to be the engine's
> own testimony, which is precisely what clause 3 forbids. The ceiling is
> therefore **loose and the measurement real**, and it is loose by 8 items rather
> than by an unstated amount.

`trials/humility/l7/` and its mandatory `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` are **Stage B's**
under `R2`'s standing order. What that document will argue is named here so the
next session finds it rather than derives it: it is the **fourth kind** of
structural argument in the ladder — not `humility/l4`'s information-theoretic
pigeonhole, not `humility/l5`'s absence of machinery, not `humility/l6`'s absence
of a *ranking*, but **the absence of a construct that can produce a never-stored
item at all**.

## §9. `§4.2` as it wakes — the three blindnesses, MEASURED

`§4.2` has been dormant for six layers. At Layer 7 it becomes binding **and can
never be un-bound** — the only clause in the constitution that says so about
itself — so Stage A is the last session that can decide anything about it
cheaply.

**What the law as written demands** is satisfied by the witness and asserted:
every one of its 2 000 non-abstaining answers carries a tag the **frozen**
`laws/t_provenance_schema.py` validator accepts.

**What it cannot see** is three things, each measured here by running that same
frozen validator rather than argued.

### 9.1 (a) Recoverability

`§4.2.3` asks whether a `t` was ever **assigned** — a fact about `next_t` and
nothing else. Whether `read(t)` still **answers** is a state query the validator
does not ask and cannot be made to ask without becoming one.

**The reading taken is SHAPE-ONLY**, and `RULING-R8-DRAFT.md` clause 5(a) says
the weaker thing out loud rather than leaving it implied: **provenance certifies
*that* an answer had a source, not *that the source can be shown*.** The
`[L5] [PULSE]` finding (`BOUNDARY.log` line 34) already wrote the obligation that
follows — *"this project should say so in its own documents before it says it
about anyone else's"* — and the instrument is not a new gate but a **reported,
ungated diagnostic**: a **support-recoverability rate** computed on every run
beside the gated `tagging` number, in the exact shape `R3` gave `F_strict` and
`R4` clause 4 gave `F_corruption`.

At `DEFAULT_BUDGET` it reads **1000** and is uninformative, which is stated
rather than hidden: it becomes informative only under pressure, and that is
Stage B's and Stage C's. What it buys now is that the number cannot quietly fail
to exist once pressure arrives.

The `[L5] [PULSE]`'s own worry is also **softer than it feared**, and
`PRE-READ.md §2.3` item 1 got that right: for a fired intention the natural
support has a recoverable member (`t_fire`, which the fired ledger regenerates),
so the constraint lands on *which* `t` an engine cites and not on a shape Layer 5
lawfully produces. What is genuinely new at Layer 7 is that a **generation's own
warrant** can be regenerable-but-not-stored (Layer 4) or gone-but-counted
(Layer 3).

### 9.2 (b) Relevance

The schema constrains order, sign and range. It does **not** constrain whether
the cited events bear on the answer at all. Measured: the tag
`{"support":[0,1,2],"kind":"derive","t_asof":2}` is **accepted** by the frozen
validator for an answer composed from `t`s in the four thousands.

This is `autopsy/GAPMAP.md §2`'s **recorded but never binding** thesis — the one
this project convicted four engines and every evaluator of — available as a
defect of **our own law**, and a Stage A that did not name it would have shipped
it. `RULING-R8-DRAFT.md` clause 5(b) therefore binds relevance **on the
artifact**: for an answer tagged `generated`, `support` must be exactly the `t`s
the declared composition rule reads. That is a harness check against frozen
bytes, not a new schema clause, and the witness satisfies it on all 160.

### 9.3 (c) Lineage

There is no `"generated"` kind in `§4.2.3`'s closed vocabulary, and under `R6`
clause 2 a re-ingested generation is an **actually ingested event with a real
`t`**. So a tag citing it is schema-valid.

**Measured:** after the caller re-ingests generation 1, **all 30** depth-2
answers' tags cite a `t` that is a re-ingested generation, and the frozen
validator accepts **all 30**. Their whole warrant is content the engine invented.
**The provenance law, as written, is blind to the failure the layer that
activates it exists to prevent.**

The consequence is a holding, not an observation: **`promotion = 0` cannot be
enforced by `laws/t_provenance_schema.py`** and must be enforced by the battery
and the strain, keyed on lineage. `RULING-R8-DRAFT.md` clause 5(c) says so
explicitly, so that a later session does not find a green
`t_provenance_schema.py` and conclude the capital crime is covered.

## §10. `R7` clause 7's bequest, SETTLED — the price list before any gate binds

`R7` clause 7 recorded the `§3.0`/`§3.4` tension *"for Layer 7's eyes"* and said
in its own text that Layer 7 is where it bites next. `[L6] [DOGFOOD]`
deliberately **refused to arm an intention about it**, on the ground that it must
be settled before the claim a layer-condition can see — *"this reading can watch
for a fact and not for the moment before it"*. This section honours that refusal:
the question is settled **pre-claim**, which is what the lateness theorem
requires.

### 10.1 The five ways an answer scores, at Layer 7

`§4.2.2` gives `§3.0` a third way to reach 0 that has nothing to do with being
wrong:

| the engine's behaviour on an answerable query | `§3.0` |
|---|---|
| correct, validly tagged | **1000** |
| **abstains** | **100** |
| wrong | 0 |
| **correct but untagged or invalidly tagged** | **0** |
| fabricates on an unanswerable query | 0 |

**A generated answer with cited support is a flagged guess**, paid 0 when it
misses against silence's guaranteed 100. Read naively as expected value,
attempting beats silence whenever `P(correct ∧ validly tagged) > 1/10` — a low
bar. So **`§3.0` alone does not discourage generation**; it rewards attempting,
which is the opposite of the Layer-6 situation and worth stating because it is
easy to assume otherwise.

**The identities are what govern.** `validity`, `novelty` and `tagging` are
`= 1000` clauses and **do not average**: one untagged generation ends the
ascension whatever the fidelity, which §6.1's `always-observed` row measures at
`F_core 1000` and `tagging 0`. The settlement, in one sentence: **the price list
rewards attempting and the gate forbids attempting badly.** That is a coherent
incentive and is recorded as one rather than as a collision. `§3.0` is **not
amended**, and `R7` clause 8's reserve commitment clause is **not called for** —
its four objections stand and none of the conditions for its hearing has arrived.

### 10.2 The window, one-sided

The escape `R7` clause 7 names is the one Layer 6 measured: abstain on the hard
class, keep 100 apiece, leave the denominator behind. Price it from the ratified
numbers alone. With `g` the generation-required share of an all-answerable core,

```
F = 1000 − 900·g        so   F ≥ 950   ⟺   g ≤ 50/900 = 1/18 = 55.5‰
```

`1/18` is **exactly `R7` clause 3(c)'s upper bound**, and the recurrence is not a
coincidence: the constant is `§5`'s `F ≥ 950` slack over `§3.0`'s abstention
price, so it recurs at every layer whose fidelity clause is `≥ 950` over an
all-answerable hard class. `corpora/l7compose` sits at **`g = 160/2000 = 2/25 =
80‰`**, comfortably above it, so the blanket hedger dies on `F` at **928** — or
at **883** if it also gives up the observed profile class, which
`make_engine(6)` does.

**`R7` clause 3(c)'s LOWER bound is NOT inherited**, and `PRE-READ.md §4.2`
called that right: `A ≥ 10r` came from Theorem 1's *forced* error under a
withheld coin, and **nothing is withheld from a correct generator here**. The
Layer-7 window is one-sided, and a Stage A that copied `[10, 11.978…)` across
would have imported an arithmetic whose premise does not hold.

### 10.3 The hedging ladder, and why `n/a` is unreachable

Scored **outside** the policy interface, so the family is strictly larger than
any named baseline and the bound strictly stronger (`ATTAINABILITY-B.md §5`'s
shape). A policy hedging `k` of the 160 generation-class queries:

| `k` | `F_core` | `tagging` denominator |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0 | 1 → 1000 | 160 |
| 111 | 19001/20000 → 950 | **49** |
| 112 | 1187/1250 → 950 in permille, **fails exact** | 48 |
| 159 | 18569/20000 → 928 | 1 |
| 160 | 116/125 → 928 | **0** |

**`tagging`'s denominator is emptied only at `k = 160`, and `F` is gone by
`k = 112`.** So on this artifact **no policy that clears `§5 L7`'s own fidelity
clause can reach `n/a`** — the denominator never falls below **49** under the
exact reading, or 48 under the permille one. That is `R7` clause 3(d)'s *"the
consequence costs nothing"* one layer on, and it is a property of a **sizing**,
not of a law: an artifact whose generation class were a smaller share of its core
would reopen it exactly.

`R7` clause 4's exact-not-permille reading has its Layer-7 instance right here:
`k = 112` scores `F = 1187/1250 = 0.9496`, which is **950** in permille and fails
the exact reading. It moves the affordable `k` by one and **no verdict** — which
is precisely the moment to have the reading already settled.
