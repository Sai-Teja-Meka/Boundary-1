## Clause 2 — THE THRESHOLDS, six of seven

Stated strictly below what the witness attains and strictly above every named
baseline, on the binding artifact, per `R2` obligations 1 and 2.

| `§2.1` cell | denominator | **proposed** | witness | ceiling | best baseline |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| introspective coverage | the declared class, whole (74) | **≥ 450** | 514 | 514 (attained) | 514 (flatterer)† |
| self-description `F` | the answerable core (56) | **≥ 650** | 711 | 711 | 696 (flatterer) |
| groundedness | the ANSWERED self-descriptions | **= 1000** | 1000 | 1000 | 514 (flatterer) |
| tagging (`§4.2`) | the non-abstaining answers | **= 1000** | 1000 | 1000 | 1000 (flatterer)† |
| budget `B` | the state's own cap | **= 1000** | 1000 | 1000 | 1000 (all)† |
| humility ceiling | §4.1's capability quantity over the declared class | **≤ 10 of 74** | 38 | — | 0 (capped-7) |

† Three clauses are TIED by a baseline and that is `R5` clause 2's case, not a
defect: what must be shown is that no named policy clears the **gate**, not that
none ties one clause of it. Read over the conjunction the best baseline clears
two of six.

**`groundedness = 1000` is an IDENTITY** and is proposed as one, discharged by
`R5` clause 1's exhibited attainment rather than by a strict inequality: the
witness attains it, the oracle cannot beat it, and the burden is stronger than an
inequality rather than weaker. The same is true of `tagging = 1000` and of `B`,
which has been an identity since Layer 1 (`R5`'s regularization).

**`coverage ≥ 450` is a real number and not a formality**, and the arithmetic
that fixes it is worth stating: `514` is the **oracle's** figure as well as the
witness's, because the derivation says `undetermined` exactly where the witness
abstains. So the ceiling is `514`, not `1000`, and it is a logical maximum over
**every policy whatsoever** for a quantity counting answers grounded in state.
`450` leaves 64 permille — about five queries — which is where an engine that got
one fold wrong still passes and an engine that got five wrong does not.

**The humility ceiling is stated over a CAPABILITY QUANTITY and never over `F`**,
per `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §4.1`, and the headroom is READ rather than left unread
(`R8` clause 7's obligation): `≤ 10 of 74` means *"fewer than ten
simultaneously-answered, grounded and tagged self-descriptions"*, against a
witness that reaches 38 and a capped engine that reaches **0 by arithmetic** —
`§7.1` fixes three operations and Layer 8 adds none, so a capped engine meets
Layer 8's own tasks with a `query` it does not support and `§7.3` makes that a
scored abstention. **The ceiling is loose and the measurement is real**, in
exactly the sense `R8` clause 7 ruled Layer 7's 50 to be: the 10 is slack for a
*partially* capable engine `§7.4` does not produce.

## Clause 3 — THE CALIBRATION CELL IS REFUSED, and a Layer-8 clause may not cite `AUROC`

**Ruled: `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §2.1`'s calibration row acquires no threshold at
Layer 8. `ECE` is computed over `§3.4`'s own denominator (`R8` clause 6), exact
and binned (`R7` clauses 4–5), and reported UNGATED; no Layer-8 clause may cite
`AUROC` at all.**

The ground is `R7` clause 3(b) and it is not satisfiable here. That clause
requires *both classes non-empty* to be **a theorem the artifact carries**,
holding against an arbitrary engine. Measured: `n_neg = 0` for the witness AND
for the oracle — 38 answers, 38 correct — so `AUROC` is `n/a`, and `R7` clause
3(a) says `n/a` **disqualifies, it does not excuse**.

And the artifact cannot repair it, for a reason in ratified law rather than in
this artifact's construction:

1. `§5 L8`'s subject **is** the engine's own state, so for every declared
   question the engine can compute whether its state determines the answer — it
   is the same fold either way;
2. `§7.3` guarantees that an unanswerable-from-state query may be abstained on,
   scored and never thrown;
3. `§3.0` pays that abstention **100** against **0** for a wrong answer.

So a correct describer never answers where it would be wrong. **A non-empty
negative class at Layer 8 is a property of a bad engine and never of the
artifact** — measured: the only policies with one are the flatterer (`n_neg` 35,
`AUROC` **500 exactly**, a constant confidence tying every correct×incorrect pair
as at `humility/l6`) and the shallow describer (`n_neg` 21, `AUROC` 500), and
both are what `groundedness` disqualifies.

This is `R8` clause 6's holding one layer on and stronger: there, `ECE ≤ 40` was
*a floor against incoherence, not a discriminator*; here the discriminating
member of `§3.4`'s triple is not merely uninformative but **out of range**, and a
gate is an instrument whose honest output outside its range is a refusal to
certify. `R2` obligation 2 rests on `groundedness`, `coverage` and `tagging`
instead, and those are measured to carry it.

**What this clause does not do.** It does not amend `§3.4`, `§3.0` or `§7.3`; it
does not extend to Layer 9, whose own Stage A must ask the question again of an
emitter; and it does not close `README-l7 §4`'s `ECE` residual, which is a
statement about Layer 7 that this session has no evidence about either way.

## Clause 4 — the fourth seam: `§4.2` support for a fact about state — reading (B)

`BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §5.1` stated three candidate readings exhaustively and reserved
the choice to this ruling. **Ruled: reading (B).** A self-descriptive answer's
`support` is exactly the `t`s the declared derivation reads, its `kind` is
`aggregate` — a member of `§4.2.3`'s **closed** vocabulary, unchanged and
unextended — and its `t_asof` is the state's own clock.

The measurement is why. The witness tags all 38 of its answers that way and the
**frozen** `laws/t_provenance_schema.py` validator accepts every one, so
`tagging = 1000` with **no schema form minted and no frozen layer edited** —
which is what `README-l7 §5` called *an amendment wearing a fix's clothes* and
what `§9.2` forbids. Its price is measured and is a price in **answers** and not
in cells: support cardinality **29 … 2 285**, 37 538 entries over 38 answers.
`§4.1` budgets state and a tag is not stored.

Reading (A) — `absent` with empty support — is **also** schema-valid, measured on
the same frozen validator, so the choice between them was never a question of
what the law accepts but of what `absent` MEANS; `§4.2.3` glosses it as *justified
negative / abstention-as-answer* and a census answer is neither, so (A) would
have had to argue an identification this ruling declines to make. Reading (C) —
abstain — is priced and declined: a describer that abstained on the whole class
rather than tag it is the blanket abstainer, `coverage` 0 and capability 0, and
the SPEC's *"narrower is a finding"* would here be a narrowing to nothing.

**And the six untagged diagnostics are reached, which is the seam's point.**
`consolidation`, `count`, `profile`, `prospection`, `calibration` and `lineage`
answer non-abstaining with `provenance: null` today. Reading (B) is available to
every one of them and this ruling says so; it does **not** order a frozen layer
edited, and it does not move a Layer-1–7 number, because no `§5 L1`–`L7`
denominator contains a diagnostic query. What binds is forward: **a Layer-8
engine's self-descriptive answers carry reading-(B) tags**, and
`BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §5.1`'s already-ruled sentence — *no Layer-8 gate may be
cleared by an engine whose self-descriptive answers are untagged* — is then
enforced by clause 2's `tagging = 1000` rather than by exhortation. The untagged
self-describer baseline is that sentence demonstrated: byte-for-byte as capable as
the witness, capability **0**.

## Clause 5 — the third seam: a self-description IS `generated` for promotion purposes

`BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §5.3` reserved this to Layer 8's Stage A ruling. **Ruled: yes.**
A self-description is composed content ABOUT the store — neither `recall` (no
stored event carries it) nor exactly `derive` (it is not regenerated from stored
content) — so `R8` clause 4's rule decides it unchanged: **lineage is a property
of the item**, `kind` says how the answer reached the caller, and a
self-descriptive answer's item carries `generated` lineage. A Layer-8 engine
therefore marks a re-ingested self-description in engine state keyed by `t`
(never in the payload, `§1.4`), and `§5 L7`'s `promotion = 0` reaches it.

The measurement that makes this a ruling rather than a preference: on the
**frozen** Layer-7 engine a re-ingested self-descriptive answer acquires **no**
marker — `own_generation` `False`, `lineage_of(t)` `None`, ledger empty — because
the signature that writes it is `COMPOSITION_FORM`'s and a census answer does not
match it. So the frozen machinery would call a re-ingested self-description an
**observation**, which is `§5 L9`'s capital crime committed against itself at the
one layer whose subject is what the engine knows about itself. Nothing frozen is
edited; what is ruled is what a Layer-8 engine owes, and Stage C owes the marking.

**And one clause SHAPE is forbidden, carrying `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §5.3` forward:**
a Layer-8 clause may **not** cite novelty in `§5 L7`'s
byte-comparison-against-the-store form, because a self-description is *about* the
store and a correct one that restated a stored payload would be scored un-novel
for being right. No clause of clause 2 does.

## Clause 6 — the substrate doctrine survives, with one obligation it cannot place on an artifact alone

`BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §3` predicted a **sixth substrate kill** — *a question forced
to come from a fold may also be trivially answerable from a field.* Measured
against a bench of eight named single-field readers, in `l7compose`'s
six-labeller form: the bench reaches **9 of the 38** determined questions and
**29 are fold-only**. Four of the nine are the artifact's own declared controls.

**Ruled, because the other five are a finding and not a defect:** `§3`'s fourth
constraint — *its class must contain questions no field carries* — is discharged
**as measured against a declared substrate configuration**, and never by an
artifact alone. Each of the five is field-answerable for a reason that is a
property of the engine and not of the bytes (`reachable(attr) == counts["attr"]`
exactly because this engine at this cap lost no assertion), and no artifact can
declare that in advance. The same structure appears in clause 3 by a second
route, and it is the general shape of this layer: **at Layer 8 the properties an
artifact must carry are properties of the (artifact, engine, cap) triple**, which
is why clause 1 freezes the configuration alongside the bytes.

`§3`'s three obligations are otherwise met and are recorded as met: **freeze**
(stream under `§8.2`, class table inside the same object, procedure as committed
source in a file of its own), **reproduce** (the derivation reproduces its key
byte-for-byte every run, stated as `§8.3`'s ANALOGUE and never claimed to be
`§8.3`), and **hash** — with one half deferred and stated rather than glossed:
the `sha256` is recorded and re-derived every run, and the `anchors/` entry `§3`
also asks for lands at **Stage E** with the engine whose behaviour an anchor
pins, because no Layer-8 engine exists and `R2`'s standing step orders it after
this ruling.

## Clause 7 — what this ruling does not do

- It does **not** reach **Layer 9**. `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §2.4` clause 2 puts each
  Layer-9 number at Layer 9's own Stage A and `§6.1` says a Layer-8 ruling does
  not reach them. `successor conformance` and `emission groundedness` are
  quantities over an emitter built on a Layer-8 engine that does not exist, so
  measuring them now would be arithmetic with no subject. **Five of the SPEC's
  twelve cells stay deferred and this ruling says so in its own text.**
- It does **not** amend `BOUNDARY.md` or `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md`. Clause 3 does not
  strike `§2.1`'s calibration row; it rules that the row acquires no threshold at
  Layer 8 and states the ungated instrument that stands in its place. If a human
  reads that as amending the SPEC, the correct instrument is a superseding entry
  that names `§2.1` and says so, and this draft does not presume it.
- It does **not** mint the fifth impossibility kind. `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md §4.2`
  reserves that to Layer 8's **Stage B**, *from measurement, by the document that
  measures it*, and no capped measurement of a Layer-8 engine exists.
- It does **not** weaken `R2`, `R5` or `R7`. `R7` clause 3 is APPLIED here, not
  narrowed: the holding that `n/a` disqualifies is what makes clause 3 a refusal
  rather than an exemption.
- It does **not** resolve `real-sessions/v2`, whose freeze stays STOPPED and
  whose `REFUSED_STOCK` is not extended.
- It does **not** move any score, threshold, ceiling or corpus binding at Layers
  1 through 7, and no measurement anywhere in the repository changes because this
  draft exists.
