# CATALOG.md — the failure catalog, and the humility axis beside it

`[L7] [PACKAGE]`, 2026-08-07. Phase 3 is complete at seven of nine layers, and
this file is what the ladder produced that a reader cannot get from
`BOUNDARY.md §5`: **a map of the ways a memory fails, with a trial name against
every entry**, and — separately, because it is a different kind of claim — **the
four ways a memory can be shown to be unable to do a thing at all.**

Two rules govern the whole file, and both are the reason it is in `packaging/`
rather than anywhere else.

* **It describes; it never redefines.** Every number, name and quotation here is
  read from the repository, and where a description and the repository ever
  disagree the repository is right and the sentence changes. `§5`'s thresholds,
  `BOUNDARY-RULINGS.md`'s entries and every frozen trial are untouched by it.
* **Every row names the trial that holds it.** A catalog whose entries are prose
  is the failure this project convicted four engines of —
  `autopsy/GAPMAP.md §2`, *recorded but never binding*. A row you cannot run is
  a row that has already started to rot.

* **Two entries were found by a PULSE audit and closed by a later session, and
  both turns of that loop found something real.** The post-L4 audit named

* **The teeth are demonstrated, never assumed.** Four rows above are made

## §2. The catalog extensions — three failures Schacter has no room for

These are not sins the catalog forgot. They are failures of a **benchmark**
rather than of a memory, and the ladder minted them because it kept measuring
itself.

### 2.1 THE DENOMINATOR LAW

> A ratio an engine reports about itself is checkable against the artifact, or it
> does not count. An empty denominator reads `n/a`, and **`n/a` disqualifies**.

`autopsy/GAPMAP.md §2`'s *evaluator* thesis, inverted and pointed at us. Its
ground is a defect this project published about somebody else:
`autopsy/writ/ANATOMY.md` records that declaring an `AdapterCapabilities`
capability `false` sets that score `null`, and `null` is filtered out of **both**
numerator and denominator (`evaluator.ts:545-548`, stated outright at
`docs/metrics.md:204`). A system exempts itself by declaring itself incapable.

It began as a **strain**: `strain/l6::trial_the_calibration_denominator_computes_exactly_under_any_abstention`
puts four abstention patterns through the shared instrument, including one that
**consults the answer key** to hedge exactly what it got right — not an engine
and it could not be, because a law about a denominator is only a law if it holds
against a pattern chosen to break it.

**AND THEN IT CHANGED TRIAL CLASS, which is the part an audit will get wrong.**
`R7` clause 3(a) ruled `n/a` disqualifying for `AUROC` on the **instrument-range**
ground — *"a gate is an instrument; a balance that reads `----` under an
out-of-range load has not weighed the object"* — and `R8` clause 3 extended it
from `AUROC`'s domain to a **general holding** over any self-reported
denominator, naming it the **fourth species** of gate clause after `R5` clause
1's identity, `R5` clause 2's minimizing clause and `R7` clause 3's empty domain.

So at Layer 7 the denominator law lives in the **ascension battery as law** and
in no strain at all:
`ascension/l7::trial_the_three_capability_ratios_are_identities_on_the_bound_denominators`,
`ascension/l7::trial_the_self_reported_denominators_are_checkable_or_the_clause_reads_n_a`
and `humility/l7::trial_the_three_capability_ratios_report_n_a_and_n_a_disqualifies`.
**An audit that looks only in `strain/` will conclude it was dropped.** It was
promoted.

Its sharpest single measurement: on `corpora/l7compose` the capped-6 engine
**ties three of seven clauses** — `promotion 0` among them, because it promotes
nothing on account of generating nothing — and read alone that row says *a
memory with no generation construct satisfies the self-pollution clause of the
generation layer.* It does. That sentence is what clause 3(c) exists to stop
anyone ending on.

### 2.2 THE NOVELTY HORIZON

> `60 → 30 → 0`, while `tagging` holds at **1000** and `promotion` stays **0**.

Quoted from `core/layers/README-l7.md §2.2`, whose own heading is *the finding no
`§5 L7` clause states*:

> *"`novelty = 1000` is "provably never-stored", a canonical-byte comparison
> against the ingested store. After the caller writes a generation back, **the
> store contains it.** Measured on the store as it now is, the byte-novel share
> of what the engine still calls generated falls*
>
> ```
> 60  ->  30  ->  0
> ```
>
> *across the three rungs, while `tagging` holds at 1000 and `promotion` stays 0.
> **At rung 3 a byte-comparison would call this engine a liar about every item it
> made.** Nothing about those items' lineage changed; what changed is where they
> have been since."*

Measured by
`strain/l7::trial_novelty_stops_being_the_right_question_at_depth_and_the_ledger_does_not`,
over the same three-generation ladder `§6`'s mandatory self-pollution strain
runs.

**It is a failure of an INSTRUMENT, and every one of the seven sins is a failure
of an ENGINE. That is the category difference, and it is why it is here rather
than in §1.** Nothing goes wrong in the engine between rung 1 and rung 3: the
items are the same items, the lineage record is the same record, and the engine's
claim about them does not move by one item. What moves is the **referent of the
measure** — the store the comparison is made against — and it moves because the
caller wrote into it, which is exactly what the strain is for.

Three consequences, and each is a design decision the arc took because of it:

* **`novelty`'s denominator is bound to what the engine TAGS, never to the
  store** (`R8` clause 3(b)). A denominator that moved with the store would make
  the clause unmeasurable at depth.
* **`promotion = 0` is a separate clause from `novelty = 1000`.** They would be
  the same clause if novelty's referent held still. It does not, so the capital
  crime needs its own number.
* **The capital crime's enforcement rides LINEAGE, not any comparison `§4.2` or
  `§3.0` can make** (`R8` clause 5(c)). `laws/t_provenance_schema.py` is
  *exactly as green against the failure as against its absence*, and a later
  session that finds it green must not conclude the crime is covered.

**The citation rule that follows, and it is not optional.** `novelty = 1000` is a
comparison against the store **at the moment of the run**. Quoting it without
saying which store it was measured against is the citation error
`packaging/HONESTY.md §6` exists to forbid, and it is the one error this measure
makes easy.

### 2.3 THE LATENESS THEOREM

> **A condition over a layer fires when the layer is CLAIMED; the store can watch
> for a fact, not for the moment before it.**

`core/layers/README-l5.md §4`'s dated note, and a **non-capability of
prospection** rather than a sin: the six-predicate condition vocabulary is over
*asserted content*, so the earliest moment a condition can name is the moment
some write asserts the fact. A **stage** — Stage A, Stage B, the session before a
claim — is not a fact any reading asserts, so it is not addressable. No
enrichment of the grammar is proposed anywhere, because `R2`'s Stage-A discipline
is to compute against the ratified gate and not to enrich it.

It has three applications, in the order they happened, and the middle one is the
most useful:

| # | the promise | what the theorem did |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`iid 1`** — armed `[L5] [DOGFOOD]` at store `t=31` on `val ≥ 6`, fired `[L6] [ASCEND]` at store `t=39`, eleven moves later | **measured, as a cost.** Its payload asked that `INTERFACE.md`'s attribute gap be stated *before the sixth shared scorer assumed it*. A scorer is written at **Stage B**, one session before a condition on a claimed layer can fire, so `trials/_l6score.py` existed before the promise could land: `trials/adapters/README.md` states the contract **after** the assumption, with the lateness recorded in it. Right about the session, wrong about the order |
| 2 | **`R7` clause 7's bequest** (*must a `§4.2` support entry be RECOVERABLE or only INGESTED?*) — **no intention armed**, `[L6] [DOGFOOD]` | **applied as a refusal, and the refusal was the useful half.** It had to be settled at Layer 7's **Stage A**, which is before any claim a condition can see, so arming it would have bought a reminder arriving after the decision it existed to inform. Carried by a document instead, and settled **on time**: `R8` clause 5(a), shape-only, before the Layer-7 claim |
| 3 | **`iid 2`** (`[L6] [DOGFOOD]`, `val ≥ 7`, fired `[L7] [ASCEND]`) and **`iid 3`** (`[L7] [DOGFOOD]`, `val ≥ 8`, pending) | **applied at arming, to choose the PAYLOAD rather than whether to arm.** A dogfood surface is upgraded **after** a claim, so the *shell* half of a question lands with a claim-conditioned promise — which is why `iid 2` landed where `iid 1` did not. `iid 3` carries only the shell half of the untagged-diagnostic seam; the engine half would be settled at Layer 8's Stage A, so it is deliberately not what that promise says |

The rule as it is now applied at arming time, from `shell/dogfood/FIELD.md`
(2026-08-03): **a promise worth arming is one whose right moment is a fact this
reading can assert, and its payload must be the half that arrives with the fact.**

The engine is unchanged and correct in all three. `§5 L5` scores **firings**, and
every one of these fired exactly where its condition was satisfied; what the
theorem governs is which conditions are worth writing. The catalog has no room
for it because absent-mindedness is *the intention not acted on*, and this is the
intention acted on exactly as written and one stage too late.

## §3. The four-kind impossibility taxonomy — FINAL, and adjacent

**This is a humility axis and it is stated beside the strain catalog, not inside
it.** The distinction is not editorial. A strain is a way an engine that has a
capability can **fail** at it; an `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` is a structural argument
that an engine which does **not** have the capability cannot reach the layer's
ceiling. One is measured against an engine under test; the other is measured
against `make_engine(layer_cap = N−1)` and argued from the constitution.

`§6` requires **every** humility trial to ship an `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` giving a
*"structural argument (not an empirical observation)"*. Six exist — `l2` through
`l7` — and **four distinct kinds** have been named across them (see the note on
`l2` and `l3` below). With Layer 7 the taxonomy has **all four kinds it will have
below `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md`**, which
is why it is frozen here and was deliberately not written at three — the post-L6
PULSE deferred this deliverable in as many words, *"writing it at three is
writing a document a single session will date."*

Naming the kind is the point. **An `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` that could be any of the
four is a template; one that says which it is has done the work.**

### The four kinds

| | layer | the kind | the one-sentence form, quoted from the document that named it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **L4** Consolidation | **ABSENT BITS** | *"thousands of distinct evicted payloads map into an aggregated forgetting record of at most 35 integer cells, so no injective map exists and the answers are unreachable **in principle**"* |
| 2 | **L5** Prospection | **ABSENT MACHINERY** | *"in budget the capped engine holds every intention **byte-exact** and still fires nothing: nothing is missing from the state, and what is missing is a verb"* |
| 3 | **L6** Meta-memory | **ABSENT ORDER** | *"a constant assigns one confidence, every correct×incorrect pair ties, `§3.4` counts a tie as ½, so `AUROC = 1/2` **exactly**. Confidence emitted is not confidence calibrated"* |
| 4 | **L7** Generation | **ABSENT GENERATIVITY** | *"an engine that can only **find** cannot **make** … **no reading of held state produces an item the store never contained**"* |

**Each one-sentence form above is quoted from
`trials/humility/l7/IMPOSSIBILITY.md §3`** — the document that *named* the four
kinds and gave the words, in the table that sets all four side by side inside the
repository (*"The four words are **absent bits (L4)**, **absent machinery (L5)**,
**absent order (L6)**, **absent generativity (L7)**"*). Two of them are verbatim in their own
layer's document as well: L6's closing line is `humility/l6/IMPOSSIBILITY.md §4`'s
own, and L4's pigeonhole is `humility/l4/IMPOSSIBILITY.md §3`'s argument in the
words `l7` gave it. This file is where the four are set beside the strain catalog
rather than beside each other.

### What makes the fourth kind different, and why the taxonomy stops at four

The first three all fail on something the capped engine lacks **about what it
holds** — the bits are gone, no verb reaches them, or nothing orders them. The
fourth is the first whose failure is **not about held state at all**: the capped
engine's information is complete, its verbs are sufficient for every question it
can answer, and its answers are ordered by a confidence model that works.

`humility/l7/IMPOSSIBILITY.md §3` distinguishes it rather than restating it, and
the two properties it names are the taxonomy's own closure argument:

* **it survives a perfect state** — nothing evicted, nothing damaged, nothing
  refused, which is exactly how the ceiling is measured, and the number does not
  move. L4's pigeonhole needed pressure; L5's needed only a verb; L6's needed a
  tie in the artifact;
* **it is closed under adding READINGS and not only under adding budget** — a
  reading is a function of the payloads and its range is bounded by them, so no
  new declared reading helps. The composition rule is a reading **plus an
  operation that emits a value the reading did not read**, and it is the second
  half Layer 6 does not have.

> The three earlier kinds ask *what is missing from the state?* This one asks
> *what is missing from the closure of the operations?*

**Why the taxonomy starts at Layer 4, and what Layers 2 and 3 are.** `§6` gives
every humility trial an `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` and there are **six** of them — `l2`
through `l7` — so the taxonomy's four kinds do not cover the class. `l2` and `l3`
predate the naming and are not a fifth and sixth kind: `l2`'s argument is that
Layer 1 has **no content→`t` index by its own README**, and `l3`'s is that the
budget law forces **fill-then-refuse** on an engine with no eviction. Both are
absences of machinery in the sense `l5` later got the word for, argued before
there was a word, and neither was re-labelled — a taxonomy applied backwards to
documents that did not claim it would be this file redefining rather than
describing.

**Why four and not five.** Layers 8 and 9 receive laws but no thresholds until
`BOUNDARY-HIGH.md` is written at the Phase 3→4 gate, and `core/layers/README-l7.md §5`
records that the Layer-8 humility side *has nowhere obvious to stand* — `§6` caps
at `N − 1` and a capped-7 engine has every verb Layer 8 will use, because `query`
is `query`. So a fifth kind is possible and no session may guess it: this
taxonomy is final **below** `BOUNDARY-HIGH.md`, and says so rather than claiming
to be final full stop.

## §4. What a competing system would have to show

Stated per axis, because the catalog is only worth publishing if it is falsifiable
by somebody else's work rather than only by ours.

| axis | what would settle it |
|---|---|
| **the seven-sin map** | a memory benchmark with a trial per sin, each measured against a **reference policy that fails it**, so the sin is shown to be hard and not merely named. Four of ours are; `autopsy/` records that neither MemoryAgentBench (`455306d`) nor WRIT (`3c0900a`) has an analogue |
| **the denominator law** | an evaluator in which declaring a capability absent **costs** the declarer. WRIT's `AdapterCapabilities` does the opposite by construction and states so in its own docs |
| **the novelty horizon** | any published novelty or hallucination-rate measure that says which **store** it was compared against, and what its own number does when the caller writes the system's output back |
| **the four-kind taxonomy** | a system frozen one capability below a claimed one, run through the identical interface on the claimed layer's own tasks, scored against a declared ceiling, with a **structural** reason it cannot exceed it. `autopsy/GAPMAP.md §4` axis (a) records that no evaluator read has the construct at all |

## §5. Where this file is checked

`trials/ops/packaging/t_catalog.py`. Every quotation above is compared against
the document it is quoted from, every trial named is required to exist under the
class this file puts it in, and the four one-sentence forms are matched against
the four `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` files. **Docs are checked, not trusted** — which is
the whole point of a `PACKAGE` move in this repository, and the only defence a
catalog has against becoming the thing it catalogs.
