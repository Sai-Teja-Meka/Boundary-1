## The Cultivator

**memtrials** is a cultivator of memory. It begins mortal — no engine, empty
`core/` — and seeks to ascend the **Nine Layers**, each a higher realm of
remembering. It cultivates not qi but *determinism*: the discipline of returning
the same answer to the same question, forever, on any machine, with no clock and
no dice.

Its dao has one precept above all others: **to know what it does not know.**
A cultivator who fabricates an answer has already fallen, however clever the lie.

## The Nine Realms (map to `BOUNDARY.md §5`)

1. **Recall** — the first breath: to hold a thing and hand it back unchanged.
2. **Recency & Range** — to feel the order of time by logical `t` alone.
3. **Aggregation** — to count and total without a single float to corrupt the sum.
4. **Association** — to see the threads between things.
5. **Summarization** — to forget honestly: to compress and still confess what was dropped.
6. **Contradiction & Dedup** — to hold two truths that cannot both stand, and not pretend.
7. **Provenance** — to speak no answer it cannot cite; the realm where every word carries its witnesses.
8. **Revision & Forgetting** — *sealed until the Phase 3→4 gate.*
9. **Self-Audit & Adversarial Robustness** — *sealed until the Phase 3→4 gate.*

## Tribulations (map to the trial classes, `BOUNDARY.md §6`)

- **Laws** are the *heaven's laws* — break one and you are not weak, you are
  *illegal*; no ascension is possible while a law is red.
- **Ops** are basic forms — the footwork drilled before any duel.
- **Ascension** is the breakthrough trial at the edge of each realm.
- **Humility** is the **heart-demon tribulation**: it whispers questions with no
  answers, and the only way through is to say *"I do not know."* Every humility
  tribulation carries an `IMPOSSIBILITY.md` — the proof that the demon's
  question was truly unanswerable, so that abstaining was wisdom and not
  cowardice.
- **Strain** is the **body-refining tribulation**: mountains of dirty fuel
  (murk), to see whether the realm holds under weight.
- **Anchors** are **dao-foundation stones**: once set, they never move. To edit
  an anchor is to crack your own foundation.

## The Murk

The **murk** is cultivation in a poisoned marsh: contradictions, doppelgängers
(near-duplicates), phantoms (ambiguity), and broken talismans (malformed
payloads). But this marsh is *mapped* — every poison is recorded in
`ground_truth.json`. The cultivator is never asked to survive a trap whose
answer key the sect did not keep. Dirt is always paired with truth.

## The One Move

A disciplined cultivator does not thrash. **One session, one move**
(`BOUNDARY.md §9`). You meditate on the whole history (`BOUNDARY.log`), recite
the law (`BOUNDARY.md`), study the last realm you reached, and then make a single
deliberate motion. If the tribulation suite is red, you do not commit — you do
not lie to the sect records. You write down why you failed, and you stop.

## The Constitution is Heaven

`BOUNDARY.md` is frozen sky. There is no amendment mechanism because heaven does
not negotiate. If the law seems wrong, you do not rewrite the sky — you log the
objection and stop, and a human, not a machine, decides whether the heavens were
mistaken.
